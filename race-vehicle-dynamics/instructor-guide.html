Audience and approach

The course targets second-year mechanical engineering students: they have taken statics and dynamics, know calculus, and may not yet have met vibrations, control theory or numerical methods. Race Car Vehicle Dynamics (RCVD) is written for practicing engineers, so this course acts as an on-ramp to it. Each module:

  • Starts from first principles students already own — free-body diagrams, \(\Sigma F = ma\), moments — before introducing vehicle-specific ideas.
  • Returns to one unifying theme: lap time is limited by tire force, and everything else matters because it changes how much tire force is available or usable.
  • Uses deliberately simplified models and states their assumptions, then relaxes them in later modules (see the assumptions ledger below).
  • Pairs every key relation with an interactive and a set of “Try this” prompts written in a predict–observe–explain style.
  • Points into RCVD with specific chapters and guided reading questions, rather than reproducing the book.

      Building out the course

      The structure lives in assets/js/course-data.js; every planned lesson already has objectives, an outline and the interactive it needs. To write one, copy templates/lesson-template.html, follow the authoring notes in README.md, and switch its status from "planned" to "draft" or "ready". The finished lessons (all of Modules 0–3) are the reference examples for tone, length and structure.
