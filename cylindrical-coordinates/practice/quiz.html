Self-Check Quiz

Fifteen questions on the core skills of Lessons 2–8: positions, unit vectors, velocity and acceleration, guided paths, equations of motion and central forces. Submit to see your score and a worked solution for every question.

How it works

  • Work on your own with a calculator and scrap paper. Try it without notes first: that shows you what you really know.
  • Nothing is marked until you press Submit quiz. Until then you can move between questions and change any answer.
  • Each question is either right or wrong. When a question has several boxes, every box must be right.
  • After you submit, you see your score, a review of every question with a worked solution, and a results sheet you can print or save as a PDF.
  • Use \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\) where gravity is needed. Angles in the formulas are in radians, and rates in rad/s.

Each box shows how it read your entry (for example = 2.356 rad (135°)), so you can check it before you submit. Type only the number; the unit is shown next to the box.

How close is close enough?

Angles are accepted within \(0.01\) rad (about \(0.6^\circ\)) of the exact value, and any equivalent angle \(\theta + 2\pi k\) counts. Give \(\theta\) with \(0 \le \theta < 2\pi\) when you can. An angle typed without ° is read in radians.

Other numbers are accepted within \(1\%\) of the exact value, so three or four significant figures are plenty. Exact forms such as 7pi/4 or 4+3sqrt(2) are always safe.
