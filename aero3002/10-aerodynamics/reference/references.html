Course texts and further reading

  1. D. P. Raymer, Aircraft Design: A Conceptual Approach, 7th ed., American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics.

    Cited in Module Home, Lesson 1, Lesson 2, Lesson 3, Lesson 4, Lesson 5, Lesson 6, Practice Lab, Self-Check Quiz, Drag Build-Up, Formula Sheet, Glossary

  2. M. Soler Arnedo (Universidad Carlos III de Madrid), Fundamentals of Aerospace Engineering, Engineering LibreTexts, CC BY-SA 3.0. Section 3.4.3, “Increase in CLmax.” Accessed Nov. 25, 2026.

    Its Table 3.1 gives the section increments of Lesson 2’s Table 2.1, credited to Franchini et al.; it writes the wing’s increment with 0.92 and the quarter-chord sweep, where [1] uses 0.9 and the hinge-line sweep.

    Cited in Lesson 2, Formula Sheet, Glossary

  3. W. H. Mason, Configuration Aerodynamics, class notes for AOE 4124, Department of Aerospace and Ocean Engineering, Virginia Tech; course page version of March 4, 2019. Accessed Nov. 25, 2026. archive.aoe.vt.edu/mason/Mason_f/ConfigAero.html

    Module 4 takes Korn’s equation with simple sweep, and its airfoil factors 0.87 and 0.95, from these notes; Lesson 7 uses them as Module 4 does.

    Cited in Lesson 7, Practice Lab, Self-Check Quiz, Drag Build-Up, Formula Sheet, Glossary

  4. Computational Design (CODE) Laboratory, Purdue University, Aircraft Design, online notes for AAE 451 Aircraft Design, 2025: “Parasitic (Zero-lift) drag” and “Drag Polar.” Accessed Nov. 25, 2026.

    The notes give Raymer’s skin-friction, form-factor, upsweep and Oswald-factor equations with their equation numbers in [1], an interference factor of 1.04 for the tails and 5% for leakage and protuberances.

    Cited in Lesson 3, Lesson 4, Lesson 5, Lesson 6, Drag Build-Up, Formula Sheet, Glossary
