How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers the drag polar, maximum lift and skin friction, Part B form factors, the build-up and the polar, and Part C wave drag and the polar in the design. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units unless a problem asks otherwise, answers to 4 significant figures: lengths in m, speeds in m/s, pressures in Pa, viscosities in Pa s, angles in degrees, and \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\). Coefficients, form factors and Reynolds numbers have no units. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
