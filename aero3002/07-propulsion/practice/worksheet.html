How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers thrust, fuel consumption, the thrust lapse and the rubber engine, Part B nacelles, engine failures, inlets and propellers, and Part C fuels, tanks, the thrust at the ceiling and the sizing loop. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units unless a problem asks otherwise, answers to 4 significant figures: thrust in N or kN, power in kW, speeds in m/s, \(c_T\) in mg/(N s) and \(C\) in h\(^{-1}\). The Formula Sheet is allowed.
