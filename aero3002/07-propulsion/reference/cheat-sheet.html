Thrust and efficiency

Momentum thrust and the efficiencies

\[ T = \dot m\,(V_j - V), \qquad \eta_p = \frac{2}{1 + V_j/V} \] \[ \eta_0 = \eta_{th}\eta_p = \frac{TV}{\dot m_f\,h}, \qquad c_T = \frac{V}{\eta_0\,h} \]
  • Piston and turboprop below about 650 km/h; turbofans at high subsonic speed (bypass ratio 2–5 business jets, 5–12 airliners); turbojets and low-bypass engines supersonic.

Fuel flow and thrust lapse

Fuel flow and the lapse with altitude

\[ \dot m_f = c_T\,T, \qquad c_T\ [\text{mg/(N s)}] = 28.33\,C\ [\text{h}^{-1}] \] \[ \alpha = \sigma \quad\text{or}\quad \alpha = \delta_0\left(1 - 0.49\sqrt M\right) \]
  • \(\delta_0 = (p/p_0)(1 + 0.2M^2)^{3.5}\), high-bypass turbofans; ram drag \(\dot m V\) cuts thrust with speed. Flat rated to a temperature; installed thrust a few percent lower.

The rubber engine

Scaling a base engine

\[ SF = \frac{T_{\text{req}}}{T_{\text{base}}} \] \[ L \propto SF^{0.4}, \quad D \propto SF^{0.5}, \quad W \propto SF^{1.1} \]
  • Keep \(SF\) within 0.8–1.25. Turbofan fit: \(W_{\text{eng}} \approx 20.17\,T^{1.005}\) kg, \(T\) in kN. FJ44-3A: 12.54 kN, 242.7 kg, 1.58 m long, 0.79 m high.

Capture area and boundary layer

Propellers

Actuator disk and tip Mach number

\[ T = 2\rho A\,v_i(V + v_i), \quad P = T(V + v_i), \quad \eta_i = \frac{V}{V + v_i} \] \[ T_{\text{static}} = \left(2\rho AP^2\right)^{1/3}, \qquad M_{\text{tip}} = \frac{\sqrt{(\pi Dn)^2 + V^2}}{a} \]
  • \(A = \pi D^2/4\); \(n\) in rev/s; \(M_{\text{tip}}\) below 0.85–0.9; real \(\eta_p\) 0.80–0.88. 25.925: 7 in to the ground, 1 in to the structure.

Common mistakes

  • Units of \(c_T\). mg/(N s) is \(10^{-6}\) kg/(N s).
  • Takeoff thrust at altitude. Multiply by the lapse \(\alpha\).
  • Weight with \(SF\). \(SF^{1.1}\), not \(SF\); diameter \(SF^{0.5}\).
  • rpm in the tip speed. Divide by 60 for rev/s.
  • The whole box as fuel. Allow for unusable fuel and expansion.
