Self-Check Quiz

Fifteen questions on the core skills of Lessons 1–8: a step of the weight loop and a cruise in steps, the minimum-drag speed and the thrust to cruise at altitude, the specific air range and the cruise-climb, the best climb speed and the time to climb, the takeoff and landing distances in the air, the manufacturing hours and labor cost of a program, the interest on an aircraft, and reading a sizing matrix. How it works

  • Work on your own with a calculator and scrap paper. Try it without notes first: that shows you what you really know.
  • Nothing is marked until you press Submit quiz. Until then you can move between questions and change any answer.
  • Each question is either right or wrong. When a question has several boxes, every box must be right.
  • After you submit, you see your score, a review of every question with a worked solution, and a results sheet you can print or save as a PDF.
  • Use SI units, \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\) and \(\rho_0 = 1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3\); costs in dollars, as each box shows.

Each box shows how it read your entry (for example = 7.2), so you can check it before you submit. Type only the number; the unit is shown next to the box.

How close is close enough?

Answers are accepted within \(1\%\) of the exact value, so three or four significant figures are plenty. Typing the calculation itself, such as sqrt(2*50000*9.81/(0.38*120*0.6726)), avoids rounding errors. Watch the units: weights in newtons in the speed formulas, Breguet with \(V\) in km/h and \(C\) per hour, angles inside tan with ° after them.
