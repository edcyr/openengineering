How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers the sizing loop, level flight and range, Part B climb, takeoff and landing, and Part C cost and trade studies. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units, answers to 4 significant figures: lengths in m, speeds in m/s, forces in N, pressures in Pa, and \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(\rho_0 = 1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3\). In Breguet's equation use \(V\) in km/h and \(C\) per hour; costs are in dollars, 2024 unless a problem says otherwise. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
