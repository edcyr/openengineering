Closing the design loop

The sizing equation, closed; cruise in steps

\[ W_0 = W_{\text{crew}} + W_{\text{payload}} + \frac{W_f}{W_0}\,W_0 + W_e(W_0) \] \[ \frac{W_f}{W_0} = 1.06\left(1 - \frac{W_{\text{end}}}{W_0}\right) \] \[ \frac{W_{i+1}}{W_i} = \exp\left(-\frac{\Delta R\,C}{V\,(L/D)_i}\right), \qquad C_{L,i} = \frac{W_i\,g}{qS} \]
  • Mission: takeoff 0.970, climb 0.985, cruise \(C = 0.65\) per h, loiter 45 min at \((L/D)_{\max}\) with \(C = 0.55\), landing 0.995. Each loop step moves 60% of the way to the new estimate.
  • Thrust loop: \(T/W\) = the largest of cruise, 300 ft/min cruise ceiling, 2.4% one engine out, 1200 m takeoff field.
  • Baseline (\(W/S = 250\), 12 km): 8898 kg at \(T/W = 0.31\); both loops 9240 kg at 0.346 (the ceiling), \(W_e = 4932\) kg, \(W_f/W_0 = 0.360\), \(S = 36.96\) m², 15.7 kN engines, cruise \(L/D = 10.43\); \(N_z = 4.83\), the gust's (4.33 from maneuvers).

Level flight and the envelope

Thrust required and available

\[ D = qS\,C_{D0} + \frac{KW^2}{qS} + qS\,C_{D,\text{wave}} \] \[ C_L^* = \sqrt{\frac{C_{D0}}{K}}, \qquad V_{md} = \sqrt{\frac{2W}{\rho S\,C_L^*}} \] \[ D_{\min} = \frac{W}{(L/D)_{\max}}, \qquad T = T_0\,\sigma \]
  • \(W\) in N. \(\sigma = \rho/\rho_0\): the thrust lapse of Module 5 (optimistic at high Mach). Maximum level speed where \(T = D\); the absolute ceiling where the low- and high-speed limits meet.
  • Baseline at 12 km, \(0.9555\,W_0\): \(V_{md} = 153\) m/s (M 0.52), \(D_{\min} = 5480\) N; Mach 0.80 leaves 560 N; \(V_{\max}\) M 0.83 (wave drag); absolute ceiling 14.25 km.

Range and endurance

Breguet, specific air range, endurance (jet)

\[ R = \frac{V}{C}\,\frac{L}{D}\,\ln\frac{W_1}{W_2}, \qquad E = \frac{1}{C}\,\frac{L}{D}\,\ln\frac{W_1}{W_2} \] \[ \text{SAR} = \frac{V\,(L/D)}{C\,W}, \qquad C_{L,\text{range}} = \sqrt{\frac{C_{D0}}{3K}} \]
  • Breguet is exact in a cruise-climb (constant \(C_L\)); at constant altitude \(L/D\) falls as fuel burns. Best endurance at \((L/D)_{\max}\). Payload-range: payload for fuel up to the takeoff weight, until the tanks are full.
  • Baseline: 4600 km at 12 km; a cruise-climb would go 5171 km (+12%). \(C_{L,\text{range}} = 0.363\), reached at Mach 0.80 near 13.9 km; 14 km saves 16% of the cruise fuel. Tanks 4.16 m³ (3328 kg) for 3329 kg: 4598 km with full payload.

Climb, ceilings and descent

Steady climb; best speeds; time to climb

\[ \sin\gamma = \frac{T - D}{W}, \qquad \text{RoC} = \frac{V(T - D)}{W} = P_s \] \[ V_{\text{RoC}} = \sqrt{\frac{W/S}{3\rho\,C_{D0}}\left[\frac{T}{W} + \sqrt{\left(\frac{T}{W}\right)^2 + 12\,C_{D0}K}\right]} \] \[ \sin\gamma_{\max} = \frac{T}{W} - \frac{1}{(L/D)_{\max}}, \qquad t = \frac{H}{R_0}\ln\frac{1}{1 - h/H} \]
  • Thrust independent of speed; steepest climb at \(V_{md}\). Ceilings: absolute 0, service 100 ft/min (0.508 m/s), cruise 300 ft/min (1.524 m/s). \(t\): RoC falling linearly from \(R_0\) to 0 at \(H\). Glide: \(\tan\gamma = 1/(L/D)\).
  • Baseline at sea level: 166 m/s, 7600 ft/min (the \(\sigma\) model; about 5000 with thrust falling with speed), \(\gamma_{\max} = 17.7^\circ\). Ceilings 14.25, 13.95, 13.45 km; 13.8 min and 219 kg to 12 km; glide about 190 km.

Takeoff and landing

Torenbeek's method

\[ \mu' = \mu + 0.72\,\frac{C_{D0}}{C_{L\max}}, \qquad s_G = \frac{V_{LOF}^2}{2g\,(T/W - \mu')} \] \[ s_A = \frac{V_{LOF}^2}{g\sqrt2} + \frac{h_{TO}}{\gamma}, \qquad \gamma = \frac{T}{W} - \frac{1}{L/D} \] \[ V_{LOF} = V_2\max\left[(1 + \gamma\sqrt2)^{-1/2},\ \tfrac{1.2}{1.3}\right] \] \[ \text{BFL} = \frac{V_{LOF}^2}{2g\,(1 + \bar\gamma/\bar a)}\left(\frac{1}{T/W - \mu'} + \frac{1}{\bar a}\right) \] \[ \qquad \times \left(1 + \frac{2g\,h_{TO}}{V_{LOF}^2}\right) + t_iV_{LOF} \] \[ s_{\text{air}} = \frac{1}{2\tan 3^\circ}\left(\frac{V_A^2 - V_{TD}^2}{2g} + h_L\right) \] \[ s_{\text{ground}} = t_iV_{TD} + \frac{V_{TD}^2}{2g\,\bar a} \]
  • \(V_2 = 1.3V_S\), \(\mu = 0.02\), \(\bar a = 0.37\) (g), \(t_i = 4.5\) s, \(\bar\gamma = 0.06 + (\gamma_{OEI} - 0.024)\) for a twin; \(h_{TO} = 35\) ft, \(h_L = 50\) ft; \(V_A = 1.3V_S\), \(V_{TD} = 1.15V_S\). Takeoff field length = max(BFL, \(1.15\,s_{TO}\)) ([3], §25.113). Mean thrust on the run 0.9 static, takeoff \(C_{D0} = 0.035\). Torenbeek [2], as implemented in AeroSandbox [5].
  • Baseline: 917 m to 35 ft, BFL 1160 m (1200 required); landing 979 m (840 required). 840 m allows \(W/S \le 202\) with \(C_{L\max,L} = 2.1\), 231.5 with 2.4.

  • \(W_e\) in kg, \(V\) the maximum speed in km/h, \(Q\) the number built; \(H_Q = 0.076\,H_M\) for cargo. Wrap rates $115, 118, 98, 108 an hour (2012); × 1.327 to 2024. Engines, avionics and interior added at their prices. Raymer [1], as implemented in AeroSandbox [5]; fitted to military aircraft, it overpredicts civil ones.

Operating cost

Per flight

\[ C_{\text{dep}} = \frac{P(1 - r_{\text{resale}})}{N_fY}, \qquad N_f = \frac{U}{t} \] \[ C_{\text{int}} = \frac{P}{N_fY}\left[\frac{iY}{1 - (1 + i)^{-Y}} - 1\right] \] \[ C_{\text{ins}} = \frac{0.025\,P}{N_f}\sqrt{\frac{n_{\text{pax}} + n_{\text{crew}} + 1}{11}}\sqrt{\frac{U}{1200}} \] \[ C_{\text{mx}} = \frac{P}{3 \times 10^6}\left[(65t + 65) + (58t + 50)\right] \] \[ C_{\text{fuel}} = m_fp_f, \quad C_{\text{crew}} = \frac{n_{\text{crew}}c_{\text{crew}}}{N_f}, \quad C_{\text{ind}} = 0.2\,C_{\text{dir}} \]
  • Fees: $1.20 per tonne of takeoff weight + $50 + $35 + $4.50 a passenger. After AeroSandbox [5], with fuel for energy and a loan repaid over the life. Business jet: 450 h a year, 2 pilots at $150 000, fuel $0.85/kg, 20 years, 40% resale, 4% real interest, 15 min taxi.
  • Baseline: $32 300 per design flight (6.41 block h): $5032 an hour, $7.02 per km, $0.877 per seat-km; depreciation and interest 37%, fixed costs 72%; $3344 an hour at 900 h a year.

Common mistakes

  • Newtons, not kilograms. \(W\) in the speed, drag and climb formulas is a force.
  • Breguet's units. \(V\) in km/h with \(C\) per hour gives km; constant altitude is not exact Breguet.
  • Thrust at altitude. \(T_0\sigma\), not \(T_0\). A jet's best range is faster than its minimum drag.
  • Field lengths. The field length is the greater of the BFL and \(1.15\,s_{TO}\); takeoff screen 35 ft, landing 50 ft.
  • Dollars. DAPCA gives 2012 dollars for the run: × 1.327, then ÷ \(Q\). Owning costs per hour fall with utilization.
