How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers weights, load factors and the wing, Part B the fuselage, landing gear, propulsion group and equipment, and Part C calibration, the sizing loop, the center of gravity and loading. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units unless a problem asks otherwise, answers to 4 significant figures: masses in kg, lengths in m, speeds in m/s, and \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\). The statistical group weight equations work in their own units: pounds, feet, square feet, inches for gear lengths, knots for stall speeds and US gallons for fuel (1 kg = 2.2046 lb, 1 m = 3.2808 ft). The Formula Sheet is allowed.
