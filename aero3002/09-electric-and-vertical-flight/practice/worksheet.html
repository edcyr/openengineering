How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on three Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers power chains, batteries and electric range, Part B sizing with a battery and hybrids, and Part C motors, blown high lift, hover and VTOL design. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units, answers to 4 significant figures: masses in kg, powers in kW, specific energies in Wh/kg (times 3600 for J/kg), jet fuel 12 000 Wh/kg, and \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(\rho_0 = 1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3\). The Formula Sheet is allowed.
