Electric propulsion systems

The efficiency chain and the power drawn

\[ \eta = \prod_i \eta_i, \qquad P_{\text{store}} = \frac{DV}{\eta} = \frac{WV}{\eta\,(L/D)} \]
  • Typical: battery 0.95, inverter 0.98, motor 0.95, propulsor 0.80 (all-electric 0.7076); generator 0.96, rectifier 0.98, gearbox 0.98, engine 0.30 (series 0.2102). Turbofan \(V/(c_T h) = 0.2968\).
  • Energy per distance \(E/R = W/(\eta\,L/D)\). All-electric business jet, 12 km, 170.5 m/s: \(TV = 662.9\), battery 936.8 kW; jet 4.20, electric 1.53 kWh/km.

Batteries

Usable specific energy, C-rate and battery mass

\[ e_b = \frac{e_{\text{cell}}\,f_{\text{use}}}{k_{\text{pack}}}, \qquad C = \frac{P}{E} \] \[ m_b = \max\!\left(\frac{E}{e_b},\ \frac{P}{C_{\max}e_b}\right) \]
  • Jet A-1: 43.2 MJ/kg = 12 000 Wh/kg, 9600 Wh/L. Packs today 150–200 Wh/kg usable; projected 650 cell → 400 pack (\(f_{\text{use}} = 0.80\), \(k_{\text{pack}} = 1.30\)) [8]. Cells about 2C today, 2–3C near term [8].
  • 1990.6 kg: fuel 23 887 kWh, battery (200 Wh/kg) 398.1 kWh. Takeoff 2355 kW = 5.92C.

Electric range and endurance

Battery range and endurance (constant weight)

\[ R = \frac{e_b\eta}{g}\,\frac{L}{D}\,\frac{m_b}{m_0}, \qquad V_{md} = \sqrt{\frac{2W}{\rho S\,\CL^*}} \]
  • \(e_b\) in J/kg (Wh/kg × 3600); \(t = R/V\). Best range at \(\LDmax\); best endurance at \(V_{mp} = V_{md}/3^{1/4}\), \(L/D = 0.866\,\LDmax\), at low altitude.
  • Business jet (\(\LDmax = 17.52\), \(\CL^* = 0.538\)), batteries 0.2867 of \(W_0\): 260.9 km at 200 Wh/kg (214.0 at M0.80); 4600 km needs 3527 Wh/kg. Jet, same fraction of fuel: 6702 km.

Sizing with a battery

Takeoff weight, battery and reserve fractions

\[ W_0 = \frac{W_{\text{pay}}}{1 - W_e/W_0 - W_b/W_0} \] \[ \frac{W_b}{W_0} = \frac{gR}{e_b\eta\,\LDmax} + \frac{gV_{mp}t_{\text{res}}}{e_b\eta\,(0.866\,\LDmax)} \]
  • Electric empty weight: \(0.9727\,W_0^{-0.06} - 0.0578 + (P/W)/p_{\text{drive}}\), with 0.30 kW/kg at 5 kW/kg: 0.5743 at 6943 kg.
  • At 6943 kg: 1976 kg of battery, 258.9 km; a 45-min reserve takes 0.2406, leaving 40.0 km. Full mission needs 3820 Wh/kg. 300 km, 20 min, 400 Wh/kg: 5000 kg.

Hybrids and distributed propulsion

Degrees of hybridization

\[ H_P = \frac{P_{\text{mot}}}{P_{\text{mot}} + P_{\text{eng}}}, \qquad H_E = \frac{E_{\text{bat}}}{E_{\text{bat}} + E_{\text{fuel}}} \]
  • 500 kg of battery for fuel: 6702 → 4859 km, \(H_E = 0.0056\). Boost for the ceiling (0.308 vs 0.284): battery 230.5 kg (3C) + motor 24.5 − engines 31.3 = +224 kg.
  • Blown lift: \((W/S)_2 = (W/S)_1\,C_{L,L2}/C_{L,L1}\). SCEPTOR: wing 158.9 → 66.7 ft², \(A\) 8.8 → 15.0 [7]. Business jet at 400 kg/m²: \(L/D\) 14.37 → 16.00 (span kept), 13.96 (\(A = 8\)).

Motors and certification

Torque, motor mass and heat

\[ P = Q\omega, \qquad \frac{m_1}{m_2} = \left(\frac{Q_1}{Q_2}\right)^{0.8587} \] \[ \dot Q = P\,\frac{1 - \eta}{\eta}, \qquad \dot m = \frac{\dot Q}{c_p\,\Delta T} \]
  • Motor weight \(\propto Q^{0.8587}\), accessories +63% [8]. 1.04 MW at 2000 rpm: 4966 N·m; at 10 000 rpm: 993 N·m; mass ratio 3.98. Drive heat at takeoff 272 kW.
  • Part 33 + special conditions 33-022-SC (magniX, 2021) [2] and 33-23-01-SC (Safran, 2025) [3], from ASTM F3338-18 [4]: duty-cycle ratings, arc faults, control faults, rotor locking, containment.

Vertical flight

Momentum theory

\[ v_h = \sqrt{\frac{T}{2\rho A}}, \qquad P = \frac{Tv_h}{FM}, \qquad \frac{T}{P} = \frac{FM}{v_h} \] \[ P_{\text{climb}} = \frac{T(V_c + v_i)}{FM}, \qquad v_i = -\frac{V_c}{2} + \sqrt{\frac{V_c^2}{4} + v_h^2} \]
  • \(P \propto 1/\sqrt\rho\); hover fuel \(Pt/(\eta_{th}h)\). \(FM\) 0.68–0.74 [8]. Tiltrotor business jet (800 N/m², 10% download): 2 × 7.72 m, 1.805 MW; climbing 1.936; hot and high 2.010 MW.

VTOL configurations and the rules

Sizing with a vertical-lift system

\[ W_0 = \frac{W_{\text{pay}}}{1 - W_e/W_0 - W_f/W_0 - f_v} \]
  • Hover: lift+cruise 1.546, tiltrotor 1.805, lift fans 5.97, jets 17.5 MW. Rotors + drive 11–18% [8]. \(f_v = 0.12\): 6666 → 16 869 kg, or 1924 km at 6943 kg.
  • SC-VTOL-01 [5]: ≤ 9 passengers, ≤ 3175 kg, unpressurized, \(M_{MO} \le 0.6\); Enhanced or Basic. FAA [6]: powered-lift a special class, SFAR No. 120.
