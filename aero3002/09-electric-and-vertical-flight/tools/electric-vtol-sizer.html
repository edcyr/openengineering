How it is calculated

  • Battery fraction (Lessons 3 and 4): \(gR/(e_b\eta\,\LDmax)\) for the range, plus a reserve flown at the minimum-power speed (\(0.760\,V_{md}\), \(L/D = 0.866\,\LDmax\)) at 1500 m at the business jet's wing loading of 248 kg/m².
  • Empty weight (Lesson 4): Module 3's jet-transport trend \(0.9727\,W_0^{-0.06}\), less the jet engines' share, plus the installed motor power per kilogram over the drive's specific power.
  • Takeoff mass: \(W_0 = W_{\text{pay}}/(1 - W_e/W_0 - W_b/W_0)\), solved for its single root; past 60 t the design is shown as not closing.
  • Battery power (Lesson 2): the installed motor power over the battery, inverter and motor efficiencies (0.95, 0.98, 0.95); C-rate = power over energy.
  • Jet (Module 3, Lesson 8): the same trend with the jet engines, Module 3's mission fractions with \(V = 850\) km/h, \(C = 0.65\)/0.55 per hour, cruise at \(0.866\,\LDmax\), a 45-minute loiter and 6% reserve; a vertical-lift system adds \(f_v\) to the empty-weight fraction.
  • Hover (Lesson 7): \(T = W(1 + \text{download})\), \(A = T/(T/A)\), \(P = T\sqrt{T/(2\rho A)}/FM\), at the hover site's density (standard atmosphere with the temperature offset).
