How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers stations, statistics and the cabin cross-section, Part B the cabin length, exits and the fuselage, and Part C the wing, tails, fuel and wetted area of a complete layout. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units unless a problem asks otherwise, answers to 4 significant figures. Seat, aisle and pitch widths are in inches (1 in = 0.0254 m); every other length is in meters. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
