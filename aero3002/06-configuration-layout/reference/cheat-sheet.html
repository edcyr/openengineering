ReferenceConfiguration Layout and Fuselage Sizing

Formula Sheet

Every key result from the module in one place. Lengths in meters unless marked in inches; the fuselage statistics are after Raymer, Chapter 6, and the cabin, fuselage, tank and wetted-area relations after Scholz's lecture notes.

Statistics and fineness

Fuselage length and seats abreast

\[ \lF = a\,\Wo^{\,C}, \qquad n_{SA} \approx 0.45\sqrt{n_{\text{PAX}}} \]
  • \(\lF\) in m, \(\Wo\) in kg. \((a, C)\): GA single 1.600, 0.23; GA twin 0.366, 0.42; twin turboprop 0.169, 0.51; jet trainer 0.333, 0.41; jet fighter 0.389, 0.39; jet transport 0.287, 0.43.
  • Fixed volume: drag least near \(\lF/\dF \approx 8\), flat from 6 to 10.

The cabin cross-section

Width and diameter

\[ \dI = \sum w_{\text{bench}} + \sum w_{\text{aisle}} + 2\,w_{\text{gap}} \] \[ \Delta d = 0.084 + 0.045\,\dI, \qquad \dF = \dI + \Delta d \]
  • Benches (in), 1/2/3 seats: economy 21/40/60, business 25/48/72; gap 1 in; 1 in = 0.0254 m.
  • Aisle (25.815), below/above 25 in: up to 10 pax 12/15, 11–19 pax 12/20, 20+ pax 15/20 in. One aisle up to 6 abreast, two up to 12 (25.817).

Cabin length, exits and crew

Cabin length

\[ l_{\text{cabin}} = n_R\,p + n_{\text{lav}}(40\ \text{in}) + n_{\text{gal}}(30\ \text{in}) + l_{\text{other}} \]
  • \(n_R = n_{\text{PAX}}/n_{SA}\), rounded up; airliners \(l_{\text{cabin}} \approx n_R(1\ \text{m})\). Pitch: first 38–40, economy 29–36, high density 30–32 in.
  • Exits each side: 1–9 IV; 10–19 III; 20–39 II + III; 40–79 I + III; 80–109 I + 2 III; 110–139 2 I + III; 140–179 2 I + 2 III.
  • Cabin crew: none to 19 seats, then 1 per 50 or part. Baggage 170, cargo 160 kg/m³.

Nose, tail cone and fuselage

Fuselage length

\[ \lF = l_{\text{cockpit}} + l_{\text{cabin}} + l_{TC} - l_{\text{overlap}} \] \[ \text{Airliner: } \lF \approx l_{\text{cabin}} + 4\ \text{m} + 1.6\,\dF \]
  • \(l_{\text{cockpit}} \approx 4\ \text{m}\); nose to full diameter \(1.7\,\dF\); \(l_{TC} \approx 3.5\,\dF\); overlap \(1.9\,\dF\) (airliner), 0 (rear engines).
  • \(\varphi_{\text{tail}} = \arctan(\dF/l_{TC}) = 15.9^\circ\) at \(3.5\,\dF\).

Wing and tails

First positions and tail areas

\[ x_{ac,W} \approx (0.92 - L_t/\lF)\,\lF \] \[ S_{HT} = \frac{c_{HT}\bar c_WS_W}{L_{HT}}, \qquad S_{VT} = \frac{c_{VT}b_WS_W}{L_{VT}} \]
  • \(L_t/\lF\): about 0.60 nose propeller, 0.52 engines on the wing, 0.45–0.48 rear engines. \(S \times L\) is fixed for a given volume coefficient.

Fuel and tanks

Fuel volume and wing tanks

\[ V_f = \frac{m_f}{\rho_f}, \qquad \rho_f \approx 800\ \text{kg/m}^3 \] \[ V_{\text{tank}} = 0.54\,\frac{S^{1.5}}{\sqrt A}\left(\frac tc\right)_{\!r}\frac{1 + \lambda\sqrt\tau + \lambda^2\tau}{(1 + \lambda)^2} \]
  • \(\tau = (t/c)_t/(t/c)_r\); no center tank. The tanks hold about 60% of the wing's volume.

Wetted area and \(\LDmax\)

Components and the refined estimate

\[ S_{\text{wet},F} = \pi\dF\lF\left(1 - \tfrac{2\dF}{\lF}\right)^{2/3}\left(1 + \tfrac{\dF^2}{\lF^2}\right) \] \[ S_{\text{wet}} = 2S_{\text{exp}}\left[1 + 0.25\left(\tfrac tc\right)_{\!r}\tfrac{1 + \tau\lambda}{1 + \lambda}\right] \] \[ \CDz = C_{fe}\frac{\Swet}{\Sref}, \qquad \LDmax = \frac12\sqrt{\frac{\pi Ae}{\CDz}} \]
  • \(S_{\text{exp}} = S - \dF(c_r + c_{\text{side}})/2\); nacelle \(\approx \pi Dl\). \(C_{fe}\): transport 0.0030, light twin 0.0045, light single 0.0055.

Common mistakes

  • Inches left in. Seat, aisle and pitch widths are in inches: multiply by 0.0254.
  • Inner for outer. \(\lF\), the tail cone and the wetted area use \(\dF\), not \(\dI\).
  • The airliner rule everywhere. Rear engines: no cabin overlap.
  • Total exits. The exit table is for each side.
  • \(S\) for \(S_{\text{exp}}\). Only the wing outside the fuselage is wetted.
  • Old arm, new fuselage. Resize the tails when \(\lF\) changes.
