How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers the design process and requirements, Part B payload–range and certification, and Part C missions, reserves and trades. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units unless a problem asks otherwise, \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(\rho = 1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3\) at sea level, answers to 4 significant figures. Latitudes are north positive and longitudes east positive; \(R_E = 6371\ \text{km}\); \(1\ \text{kt} = 0.5144\ \text{m/s}\), \(1\ \text{nmi} = 1.852\ \text{km}\). Aircraft and missions are illustrative. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
