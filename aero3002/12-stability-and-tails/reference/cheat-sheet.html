Formula Sheet

Every key result from the module in one place. Derivatives are per radian unless marked; positions along the mean aerodynamic chord are fractions of it from its leading edge; moment coefficients use the wing's area and chord (its span for yaw and roll).

Fits on two pages of Letter or A4: pitch on the first, yaw, roll and engine failure on the second. For a digital copy, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer.

Static stability

Stable and trimmable; static margin

\[ C_{M\alpha} \lt 0, \qquad C_{M0} \gt 0 \] \[ \text{SM} = \bar x_{np} - \bar x_{cg}, \qquad C_{M\alpha} = -C_{L\alpha}\,\text{SM} \]
  • \(\bar x\): along the MAC, as a fraction of it from its leading edge.
  • Required at the aft CG: 5% for business jets, regional turboprops and jet transports; 10% for light propeller aircraft (source: Scholz, after Roskam). Engines take 1–3% (jets), 4–10% (propellers).

Pitching-moment contributions

Wing, fuselage, downwash, engines

\[ C_{M,W} = c_{m,0}\,\frac{A\cos^2\Lambda_{c/4}}{A + 2\cos\Lambda_{c/4}} \] \[ C_{M\alpha,\text{fus}} = \frac{K_fW_f^2L_f}{\cbar S}\ \text{per deg} \] \[ K_f = 0.01 - 0.063\,x_0 + 0.211\,x_0^2 \] \[ \frac{\partial\varepsilon}{\partial\alpha} = 4.44\left[k_Ak_\lambda k_H\sqrt{\cos\Lambda_{c/4}}\right]^{1.19}\frac{C_{L\alpha}(M)}{C_{L\alpha}(0)} \] \[ k_A = \frac1A - \frac{1}{1 + A^{1.7}}, \quad k_\lambda = \frac{10 - 3\lambda}{7} \] \[ k_H = \frac{1 - |z_H/b|}{\sqrt[3]{2l_H/b}}, \qquad C_{M,E} = -\frac{Tz_E}{qS\cbar} \]
  • \(x_0\): wing root quarter-chord / \(L_f\), 0.1 to 0.62. \(\eta_H = 0.9\) (0.85–0.95). \(z_E\) positive above the CG.
  • Business jet: \(c_{m,0} = -0.10\) (assumed), \(C_{M,W} = -0.067\); \(C_{L\alpha,W} = 4.507\) (M 0.2), 5.969 (M 0.8); \(C_{M\alpha,\text{fus}} = 0.707\); \(\partial\varepsilon/\partial\alpha = 0.335\) (M 0.2) to 0.443 (M 0.8).

The neutral point

Stick-fixed neutral point

\[ T = \eta_H\frac{S_H}{S}C_{L\alpha,H}\left(1 - \frac{\partial\varepsilon}{\partial\alpha}\right) \] \[ \bar x_{np} = \frac{C_{L\alpha,W}\bar x_{ac,W} - C_{M\alpha,\text{fus}} + T\bar x_{ac,H}}{C_{L\alpha,W} + T} \] \[ C_{M\alpha} = -(C_{L\alpha,W} + T)(\bar x_{np} - \bar x_{cg}) \]
  • \(\bar x_{ac,W} \approx 0.25\); \(\bar x_{ac,H}\) in MACs from the MAC's leading edge. Higher Mach moves the neutral point forward: cruise decides the aft limit.
  • Business jet, Module 11's layout: 41.0% (low speed), 37.6% (cruise); margin at the aft CG 8.1% and 4.7%.

Trim and the elevator

Tail lift for trim; control-surface effectiveness

\[ C_{L,H} = \frac{C_{M,W} + C_L\bar x_{cg-ac} + C_{M,E}}{\eta_H(S_H/S)(l_H/\cbar)} \] \[ \tau = 1.576\,c_r^3 - 3.458\,c_r^2 + 2.882\,c_r, \quad c_r = \frac{c_f}{c} \] \[ \Delta C_{L,H} = C_{L\alpha,H}\,\tau\,\delta_e \]
  • \(\bar x_{cg-ac} = \bar x_{cg} - 0.25\). Wing lift \(C_{L,W} = C_L - \eta_H(S_H/S)C_{L,H}\); induced drag \(\times(C_{L,W}/C_L)^2\).
  • \(\tau = 0.596\) for a 30% elevator or rudder. Business jet in cruise, forward CG: \(C_{L,H} = -0.138\), wing 110.3% of the lift.

Control at the forward CG

The control line

\[ \frac{S_H}{S} = \frac{C_L\bar x_{cg-ac} + C_{M,W} + C_{M,E}}{C_{L,H}\,\eta_H\,(l_H/\cbar)}, \quad C_{L,H} = -0.5 \] \[ \Delta c_m = \Delta c_l\left(0.25 - 0.44\,\frac{c'}{c}\right) \]
  • Flapped wing: \(c_{m,0} + (S_{\text{flapped}}/S)\Delta c_m\), times the wing factor (the flapped-share scaling is this module's assumption).
  • Rear engines: go-around at \(1.3V_S\), \(C_L = C_{L\max}/1.69\), takeoff thrust (assumed); engines under the wing: landing at \(C_{L\max}\), idle. Business jet at Module 11's forward CG: 7.89 m².

The scissor plot

The stability line; the smallest tail

\[ \frac{S_H}{S} = \frac{C_{L\alpha,W}(\bar x_{cg} + \text{SM} - \bar x_{ac,W}) + C_{M\alpha,\text{fus}}}{\eta_HC_{L\alpha,H}\left(1 - \frac{\partial\varepsilon}{\partial\alpha}\right)(\bar x_{ac,H} - \bar x_{cg} - \text{SM})} \] \[ \bar x_{\text{aft}}(S_H) - \bar x_{\text{fwd}}(S_H) = \text{width of the loading range} \] \[ c_{HT} = \frac{S_Hl_H}{S\cbar} \]
  • Check the stability line at low speed and in cruise; the larger tail holds. Move the wing to put the loading range where the blades open, and iterate.
  • Business jet: \(S_H = 6.75\) m² (\(c_{HT} = 0.82\)), wing quarter-MAC 7.405 m (47.8%), CG 21.5–37.1%, neutral point 46.7% / 42.1%.

Directional and lateral stability

\(C_{n\beta}\) and \(C_{l\beta}\), per radian

\[ C_{n\beta} = C_{n\beta,W} + C_{n\beta,F} + C_{n\beta,V}, \qquad C_{n\beta,W} = \frac{C_L^2}{4\pi A} \] \[ a_V = C_{L\alpha,V}\left(1 + \frac{\partial\sigma}{\partial\beta}\right)\eta_V\frac{S_V}{S} \] \[ C_{n\beta,V} = a_V\frac{l_V}{b}, \qquad C_{l\beta,V} = -a_V\frac{z_V}{b} \] \[ \begin{aligned} \left(1 + \frac{\partial\sigma}{\partial\beta}\right)\eta_V = 0.724 + \frac{3.06\,S_V/S}{1 + \cos\Lambda_{c/4}} \\ + 0.4\,\frac{z_w}{d} + 0.009A \end{aligned} \] \[ C_{n\beta,F} = -1.3\,\frac{\text{Vol}_F}{Sb}\,\frac dw \ \text{(volume)} \] \[ C_{n\beta,F} = -\frac{180}{\pi}\,k_Nk_{Rl}\,\frac{l_F^2d}{Sb} \ \text{(DATCOM)} \] \[ k_N = 0.01\left[0.27\frac{x_{cg}}{l_F} - 0.168\ln\frac{l_F}{d} + 0.416\right] - 0.0005 \] \[ k_{Rl} = 0.46\log_{10}(Re_{l_F}/10^6) + 1, \qquad C_{n\beta} \ge 0.0571 \] \[ C_{l\beta,\Gamma} = -\frac{C_{L\alpha,W}\Gamma}{4}\cdot\frac{2(1 + 2\lambda)}{3(1 + \lambda)} \] \[ C_{l\beta,WF} = 1.2\sqrt A\,\frac{z_w}{b}\,\frac{d + w}{b} \]
  • Fin lift slope from Module 4's formula with \(A_{\text{eff}} = 1.55A_V\) (assumed). \(z_w\) positive below the axis (low wing). \(C_{l\beta} \lt 0\) is stable; the sweep term is not included.
  • Business jet, 3.67 m² fin: \(C_{n\beta} = 0.113\) (volume) or 0.064 (DATCOM); fin needed 2.66 or 3.56 m²; \(C_{l\beta} = -0.071\).

Engine failure and crosswind

Rudder at \(V_{MC}\); fin area; crosswind

\[ N_E + N_D = (1 + k_D)\,T\,y_E, \qquad V_{MC} \le 1.13\,V_{SR} \] \[ C_{n\delta_r} = 0.9\,C_{L\alpha,V}\frac{S_V}{S}\frac{l_V}{b}\tau_r, \qquad \delta_r = \frac{N_E + N_D}{qSb\,C_{n\delta_r}} \] \[ S_V \ge \frac{N_E + N_D}{0.9\,q\,l_V\,C_{L\alpha,V}\,\tau_r\,\delta_{r,\max}}, \qquad \delta_r = \frac{C_{n\beta}\beta}{C_{n\delta_r}} \]
  • \(k_D\) = 0.15 low bypass, 0.25 high bypass; \(\delta_{r,\max} \approx 25^\circ\). Crosswind 20% of takeoff speed: \(\beta = 11.3^\circ\), rudder \(\le 20^\circ\). The fin takes the largest requirement.
  • Business jet: \(V_{MC} = 54.6\) m/s, rudder \(14.9^\circ\), fin needed 2.18 m²; crosswind \(17.9^\circ\). Resized tails: \(C_{D0} = 0.0207\), \((L/D)_{\max} = 15.67\), \(\Wo\) +172 kg in one turn of the loop.

Common mistakes

  • Per degree, per radian. \(K_f\) gives per degree; multiply by \(180/\pi\). Angles enter derivatives in radians.
  • Positions on the MAC. \(\bar x_{cg-ac} = \bar x_{cg} - 0.25\), not \(\bar x_{cg}\).
  • Signs. The fuselage destabilizes: \(C_{M\alpha,\text{fus}} \gt 0\), \(C_{n\beta,F} \lt 0\). Stable: \(C_{M\alpha} \lt 0\), \(C_{n\beta} \gt 0\), \(C_{l\beta} \lt 0\).
  • Downwash. The tail sees only \(1 - \partial\varepsilon/\partial\alpha\) of each degree.
  • Which speed. Stability in cruise, control at low speed with flaps; the windmilling engine's drag adds to the live one's thrust.
