The two ratios

Definitions and sizes

\[ \frac TW = \frac{T_0}{\Wo g}, \qquad \frac WS = \frac{\Wo}{S}, \qquad \frac PW = \frac{P_0}{\Wo} \] \[ S = \frac{\Wo}{\WSo}, \qquad T_0 = \TWo\,\Wo\,g \]
  • \(W/S\) in kg/m², \(P/W\) in kW/kg; \(T_0\), \(P_0\): total sea-level static. kg/m² \(\times 9.81\) = N/m²; \(\div 4.882\) = lb/ft². Typical \(W/S\): GA single 83, twin turboprop 195, jet trainer 244, jet transport 586 kg/m² [1].

Statistics and thrust matching

Fits, weight fraction, thrust lapse

\[ \TWo = aM_{\max}^{\,C}, \qquad \TWo = \frac{\beta}{\alpha}\,\frac{1}{(L/D)_{\text{cruise}}} \] \[ \left(\frac PW\right)_0 = \frac{gV}{1000\,\eta_p}\,\frac{\beta}{\alpha_P}\,\frac{1}{L/D} \]
  • \(\beta = W/\Wo\), \(\alpha = T/T_0 \approx \sigma\) (jets, turboprops); piston \(\alpha_P = \sigma - (1 - \sigma)/7.55\).
  • \((a, C)\): trainer 0.488, 0.728; dogfighter 0.648, 0.594; fighter 0.514, 0.141; cargo/bomber 0.244, 0.341; jet transport 0.267, 0.363 [1].

Stall and landing

Stall speed and landing distance

\[ \frac WS = \frac{\rho V_s^2\CLmax}{2g}, \qquad V_{\text{REF}} = 1.23\,V_{SR} \] \[ s_L = 4.996\,\frac{(W/S)_L}{\sigma C_{L_{\max},L}} + S_a \] \[ \WSo \le \frac{(s_L - S_a)\,\sigma\,C_{L_{\max},L}}{4.996\,W_L/\Wo} \]
  • \(S_a\): 305 m airliner, 183 m light, 137 m STOL [1]. Reversers: first term \(\times 0.66\). Field length \(= s_L/0.6\) [9].

Takeoff

Takeoff parameter (statistical fits)

\[ \text{TOP} = \frac{W/S}{\sigma\,C_{L_{\max},TO}\,(T/W)}, \qquad s_{TO} = 2.34\,\text{TOP} \] \[ \TWo \ge \frac{2.34\,\WSo}{s_{TO}\,\sigma\,C_{L_{\max},TO}} \] \[ \text{TOP}_P = \frac{W/S}{\sigma\,C_{L_{\max},TO}\,(P/W)} \] \[ s_G = 0.503\,\text{TOP}_P + 3.11 \times 10^{-4}\,\text{TOP}_P^{\,2} \]
  • Jets [10]: \(W/S\) in kg/m², \(s_{TO}\) in m, scatter about \(\pm\)10 to 15%. Light propeller aircraft [2]: \(P/W\) in kW/kg, ground roll \(s_G\) in m. \(C_{L_{\max},TO} \approx 0.8\,C_{L_{\max},L}\).

Cruise, climb, ceiling, turns

The master equation

\[ \begin{aligned} \TWo = \frac{\beta}{\alpha}\Big[&\frac{q\CDz}{\beta g\WSo} + \frac{n^2K\beta g\WSo}{q} \\ &+ \frac1V\frac{\dd h}{\dd t}\Big] \end{aligned} \] \[ q = 0.7pM^2, \qquad K = \frac{1}{\pi Ae} \]
  • Cruise: \(n = 1\). Ceiling: 100 ft/min service, 300 ft/min cruise (0.51, 1.52 m/s). Instantaneous turn: \(\WSo \le q\CLmax/(ng\beta)\).

Best wing loadings

At the start of the segment, \(\WS = qC_L/g\)

\[ \begin{aligned} \text{Jet range: } & C_L = \sqrt{\CDz/(3K)} \\ \text{Jet loiter, prop range: } & C_L = \sqrt{\CDz/K} \\ \text{Prop loiter: } & C_L = \sqrt{3\CDz/K} \end{aligned} \]
  • Divide by \(\beta\) for \(\WSo\). Least cruise thrust at \(\LDmax\), \(C_L^{*} = \sqrt{\CDz/K}\).

Engine out

Second segment, one of \(N\) engines failed

\[ \TWo = \frac{N}{N - 1}\left(\frac{1}{L/D} + G\right) \]
  • Minimum \(G\): 2.4% (two engines), 2.7% (three), 3.0% (four) [8]. \(L/D\) with takeoff flaps, gear up. A horizontal line.

The design point

  • Feasible: above every \(\TW\) curve, left of every \(\WS\) limit.
  • Design point: the lowest \(\TW\) in the region, at the highest \(\WS\) that gives it; often a corner where two constraints are active.
  • New \(\Wo\): keep the ratios, rescale \(S\) and \(T_0\).

Common mistakes

  • Mass for weight. \(T/W\) divides by \(\Wo g\), in newtons.
  • kg/m² in the master equation. Multiply by \(g\) (and \(\beta\)) first.
  • Forgetting the lapse. In flight, divide by \(\alpha\) for sea-level thrust.
  • Takeoff weight in the landing. Use \((W/S)_L\), then divide by \(W_L/\Wo\).
  • km/h in \(\rho V^2\). Convert to m/s.
  • One engine's thrust. \(T_0\) is the total of all engines.
