How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers the two ratios, statistics and thrust matching, Part B stall, landing and takeoff, and Part C cruise, climb, ceiling, turns and a complete constraint analysis. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units unless a problem asks otherwise, \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(\rho_0 = 1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3\), \(\gamma = 1.4\), answers to 4 significant figures. Wing loadings are in kg/m² of takeoff mass; multiply by \(g\) for N/m² inside the master equation. Thrust-to-weight ratios refer to the takeoff weight and the total sea-level static thrust. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
