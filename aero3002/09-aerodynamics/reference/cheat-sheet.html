ReferenceLift and the Drag Build-Up

Formula Sheet

Every key result from the module in one place, in SI units. Coefficients refer to the wing's reference area; lengths for Reynolds numbers are each component's own.

Fits on one page of Letter or A4. For a digital copy, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer.

The drag polar

Parabolic polar and the best points

\[ C_D = C_{D0} + KC_L^2, \qquad K = \frac{1}{\pi Ae} \] \[ C_L^* = \sqrt{\frac{C_{D0}}{K}}, \qquad \left(\frac LD\right)_{\max} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{C_{D0}K}} \] \[ C_L = \frac{W}{qS}, \qquad q = 0.7pM^2 \]
  • Jet's best range at \(C_L^*/\sqrt3\), with \(L/D = 0.866\,(L/D)_{\max}\).

Maximum lift

Clean wing and devices

\[ \CLmax \approx 0.9\,c_{l,\max}\cos\Lambda_{c/4} \] \[ \Delta\CLmax = 0.9\,\Delta c_{l,\max}\,\frac{S_{\text{flapped}}}{\Sref}\cos\Lambda_{\text{H.L.}} \]
  • \(\Delta c_{l,\max}\): plain 0.9, slotted 1.3, Fowler \(1.3\,c'/c\), double-slotted \(1.6\,c'/c\), triple \(1.9\,c'/c\); slot 0.2, Krueger 0.3, slat \(0.4\,c'/c\) (source: Raymer, Table 12.2).

Skin friction

Reynolds number and flat-plate friction

\[ Re = \frac{\rho Vl}{\mu}, \qquad \mu = \frac{1.458\times10^{-6}T^{1.5}}{T + 110.4} \] \[ C_{f,\text{lam}} = \frac{1.328}{\sqrt{Re}} \] \[ C_{f,\text{turb}} = \frac{0.455}{(\log_{10}Re)^{2.58}(1 + 0.144M^2)^{0.65}} \] \[ Re_{\text{cutoff}} = 38.21\,(l/k)^{1.053} \]
  • Laminar share \(f\): \(C_f = fC_{f,\text{lam}} + (1 - f)C_{f,\text{turb}}\). Use \(Re_{\text{cutoff}}\) if it is lower than \(Re\).

Form and interference

Form factors

\[ FF_{\text{wing}} = \left[1 + \frac{0.6}{(x/c)_m}\frac tc + 100\left(\frac tc\right)^4\right] \] \[ \times\,1.34M^{0.18}\cos^{0.28}\Lambda_m \] \[ FF_{\text{fus}} = 0.9 + \frac{5}{f^{1.5}} + \frac{f}{400}, \qquad FF_{\text{nac}} = 1 + \frac{0.35}{f} \]
  • \(Q\): fuselage and filleted wing 1.0; unfilleted low wing 1.1–1.4; tails 1.04–1.05; nacelles 1.5 on a surface, 1.3 within a diameter, 1.0 beyond.

The build-up

Zero-lift drag

\[ C_{D0} = \frac{\sum C_f\,FF\,Q\,\Swet}{\Sref} + C_{D,\text{misc}} + C_{D,\text{L\&P}} \] \[ \left(\frac Dq\right)_{\text{upsweep}} = 3.83\,u^{2.5}A_{\max}, \qquad C_{fe} = \frac{C_{D0}}{\Swet/\Sref} \]
  • Upsweep \(u\) in radians; leakage and protuberances a few percent (5% here).

Induced drag

Oswald factor

\[ e = 1.78\left(1 - 0.045A^{0.68}\right) - 0.64 \]
  • For sweep below about 30°; not for very high aspect ratios. At \(C_L^*\), \(C_{D0} = KC_L^2\).

Wave drag

Korn and the drag rise

\[ \MDD = \frac{\kappa_A}{\cos\Lambda} - \frac{t/c}{\cos^2\Lambda} - \frac{C_L}{10\cos^3\Lambda} \] \[ M_{\text{crit}} = \MDD - 0.108, \qquad \Delta C_{D,\text{wave}} = 20(M - M_{\text{crit}})^4 \]
  • \(\kappa_A\): 0.87 conventional, 0.95 supercritical. Jet range factor \(M\,L/D\).

Takeoff, landing, cruise

Configuration drag

\[ \Delta C_{D0,\text{flap}} = F\frac{c_f}{c}\frac{S_{\text{flapped}}}{\Sref}(\delta_f - 10^\circ) \] \[ \Delta C_{D0,\text{gear}} = \frac{1.2\sum(D/q)_i}{\Sref} \]
  • \(F\): 0.0074 slotted and Fowler, 0.0144 plain. Gear \(D/q\): 0.25 per wheel's frontal area, 0.30 per round strut's. \(e\) falls by 0.05 (takeoff flaps) or 0.10 (landing). \(V_2\): \(C_L = \CLmax/1.44\); approach: \(\CLmax/1.69\).

Common mistakes

  • Which length. MAC for wings and tails, body length for fuselages and nacelles.
  • Which sweep. \(\Lambda_m\) in the form factor, the hinge line's for flaps.
  • The factor 2. \((L/D)_{\max} = 1/(2\sqrt{C_{D0}K})\).
  • Leakage. A share of the components and the miscellaneous drags together.
  • Cruise \(C_L\). From \(W/(qS)\), not from the best \(L/D\).
