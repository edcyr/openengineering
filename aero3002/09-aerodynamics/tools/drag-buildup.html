How it is calculated

  • Geometry (Module 6): wetted areas, lengths, thickness ratios and sweeps from the chosen layout (the business jet with Module 7's nacelles). The length is the mean aerodynamic chord for the wing and tails, the body length for the fuselage and nacelles.
  • Skin friction (Lesson 3): \(Re = \rho Vl/\mu\) at the cruise altitude and Mach number; \(C_f = 1.328/\sqrt{Re}\) laminar and \(0.455/[(\log_{10}Re)^{2.58}(1 + 0.144M^2)^{0.65}]\) turbulent, mixed by the laminar share on the wing and tails, with \(Re\) capped at \(38.21(l/k)^{1.053}\) for the surface chosen.
  • Form factors and interference (Lesson 4): wings and tails \([1 + 0.6(t/c)/(x/c)_m + 100(t/c)^4][1.34M^{0.18}\cos^{0.28}\Lambda_m]\); fuselage \(0.9 + 5/f^{1.5} + f/400\); nacelles \(1 + 0.35/f\); \(Q\) 1.04 for the tails and as chosen for the wing and nacelles.
  • Miscellaneous (Lesson 5): upsweep \(3.83u^{2.5}A_{\max}\) from the layout's tail cone; leakage and protuberances as a share of the rest.
  • Polar (Lesson 6): \(e = 1.78(1 - 0.045A^{0.68}) - 0.64\), \(K = 1/(\pi Ae)\); \((L/D)_{\max}\), \(C_L^*\) and the best-range point at \(C_L^*/\sqrt3\). Cruise: \(C_L = (W/S)/(0.7pM^2)\) at the weight fraction chosen.
  • Drag rise (Lesson 7): Korn's \(\MDD\) at the cruise \(C_L\), \(M_{\text{crit}} = \MDD - 0.108\), wave drag \(20(M - M_{\text{crit}})^4\). Clean \(\CLmax = 0.9\,c_{l,\max}\cos\Lambda\) (Lesson 2).
  • The business jet preset is the module's; the other three are illustrative, not real types. Several constants (the Mach correction of the friction, the roughness heights, the leakage allowance, the wave-drag rise) are typical values to check. Your inputs are saved in this browser.
