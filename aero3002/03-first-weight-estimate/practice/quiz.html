Self-Check Quiz

Fifteen questions on the core skills of Lessons 1–7: the sizing equation, empty-weight trends and their units, wetted area and \(\LDmax\), fuel consumption of jets and propellers, segment and mission fractions, the iteration and payload drops, and growth factors. How it works

  • Work on your own with a calculator and scrap paper. Try it without notes first: that shows you what you really know.
  • Nothing is marked until you press Submit quiz. Until then you can move between questions and change any answer.
  • Each question is either right or wrong. When a question has several boxes, every box must be right.
  • After you submit, you see your score, a review of every question with a worked solution, and a results sheet you can print or save as a PDF.
  • Use \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(\rho = 1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3\) at sea level, \(1\ \text{lb} = 0.4536\ \text{kg}\) and \(1\ \text{lb/(hp h)} = 0.6083\ \text{kg/(kW h)}\), unless a question says otherwise.

Each box shows how it read your entry (for example = 7.2), so you can check it before you submit. Type only the number; the unit is shown next to the box.

How close is close enough?

Answers are accepted within \(1\%\) of the exact value, so three or four significant figures are plenty. Typing the calculation itself, such as 0.55*0.6083*9.81*(450/3.6)/(1000*0.82), avoids rounding errors. Watch the units: the trend coefficient in lb or kg, minutes to hours when \(C\) is per hour, and km/h to m/s in the propeller \(C\).
