How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers the sizing equation and empty-weight trends, Part B \(L/D\) and fuel consumption, and Part C fuel fractions, closing the loop and sensitivities. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units unless a problem asks otherwise, \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), answers to 4 significant figures. Convert with \(1\ \text{lb} = 0.4536\ \text{kg}\) and \(1\ \text{lb/(hp h)} = 0.6083\ \text{kg/(kW h)}\). Jets cruise at \(0.866\,\LDmax\) and loiter at \(\LDmax\); propeller aircraft the other way round. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
