How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers placing the gear, its angles and its loads, Part B tires, shock absorbers and stowage, and Part C hydraulic and electrical power, pressurization and fresh air. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units unless a problem asks otherwise, answers to 4 significant figures: loads in N or kN (tires in lb, 1 lbf = 4.448 N), lengths in m (tires in inches), pressures in Pa or MPa (tires in psi, 1 psi = 6895 Pa), and \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\). The Formula Sheet is allowed.
