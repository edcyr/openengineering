Self-Check Quiz

Fifteen questions on the core skills of Lessons 1–8: the gear arrangements, the wheelbase, tail-down and tip-over angles, the static and braking gear loads, tire sizes, ratings and deflection, the shock absorber's stroke and the oleo strut, the rules for a retractable gear, a hydraulic actuator, and the cabin's hoop stress and fresh air. How it works

  • Work on your own with a calculator and scrap paper. Try it without notes first: that shows you what you really know.
  • Nothing is marked until you press Submit quiz. Until then you can move between questions and change any answer.
  • Each question is either right or wrong. When a question has several boxes, every box must be right.
  • After you submit, you see your score, a review of every question with a worked solution, and a results sheet you can print or save as a PDF.
  • Use SI units, except for tires, whose loads, pressures and sizes the catalogs give in pounds, psi and inches; each box shows the unit it expects.

Each box shows how it read your entry (for example = 7.2), so you can check it before you submit. Type only the number; the unit is shown next to the box.

How close is close enough?

Answers are accepted within \(1\%\) of the exact value, so three or four significant figures are plenty. Typing the calculation itself, such as 176580*(1.4+0.31*2)/9/1000, avoids rounding errors. Watch the units: tire statistics take pounds and give inches (1 lbf = 4.448 N, 1 in = 25.4 mm), 10 ft/s is 3.048 m/s, 1 psi is 6895 Pa, and atan gives radians.
