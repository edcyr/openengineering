Arrangements

The ground loop

\[ N = F_y\,(x_m - x_{cg}) \]
  • Main wheels behind the CG (tricycle): a swerve dies out; ahead of it (taildragger): it grows. Arrangements: single main, taildragger, tricycle, bicycle, quadricycle, multi-bogey.

Placing the gear

Side-view guidelines

\[ \tan\theta_{TD} = \frac{h}{x - x_m}, \qquad \tan\theta_{tb} = \frac{x_m - x_{cg,a}}{h_{cg}} \] \[ \frac{W_n}{W} = \frac{x_m - x_{cg}}{B}, \qquad B \ge \frac{x_{cg,a} - x_{cg,f}}{\Delta(W_n/W)} \]
  • \(\theta_{TD}\) 10–15°; \(\theta_{tb} \ge 15^\circ\) and \(> \theta_{TD}\); nose share 8–15%, never below 5% or above 20% [1], [7].

Tip-over and clearance

The lateral tip-over angle

\[ \tan\delta = \frac{y_m}{B}, \qquad d = (x_{cg} - x_n)\sin\delta, \qquad \tan\psi = \frac{h_{cg}}{d} \]
  • \(\psi \le 63^\circ\) at the forward CG (54° carrier aircraft) [1]. Bank to touch the ground > 5°; nacelle about \(0.25\) fan diameter above the ground [2]; propeller ≥ 7 in (CS/FAR 25.925 [3], [4]).

Gear loads

Static and braking loads

\[ W_{m,\max} = W\frac{x_{cg,a} - x_n}{B}, \qquad W_{n,\max} = W\frac{x_m - x_{cg,f}}{B} \] \[ W_{n,\min} = W\frac{x_m - x_{cg,a}}{B} \] \[ W_{n,\text{brake}} = W\frac{(x_m - x_{cg,f}) + (a/g)h_{cg}}{B} \]
  • Nose tire checked at \(a = 0.31\,g\). About 20 t per main wheel on narrow-bodies; ACN about twice the single-wheel load in tonnes [2].

Tires

Size, rating and footprint

\[ D \text{ or } w = A\,W_w^{\,B}\ \ (\text{in, lb}), \qquad p = p_{\text{rated}}\frac{W_w}{W_{\text{rated}}} \] \[ W_w \approx p\,A_p, \qquad S_T = \frac{D}{2} - R_{\text{SLR}} \]
  • Business twin \(D\): 2.69, 0.251; \(w\): 1.170, 0.216 ([1], Table 11.1). Main: load ≤ rated (× 1.07 on multi-wheel axles); nose: static ≤ rated, braking ≤ 1.5 × rated (CS/FAR 25.733 [3], [4]).

Shock absorbers

Stroke and oleo

\[ \eta = \frac{\int F\,\mathrm{d}s}{F_{\max}S}, \qquad S = \frac{V_v^2}{2g\,\eta N_g} - \frac{\eta_T}{\eta}S_T \] \[ d_p = \sqrt{\frac{4L}{\pi P}}, \quad D_{\text{oleo}} \approx 1.3\,d_p, \quad L_{\text{oleo}} \approx 2.5\,S \]
  • \(V_v\) = 10 ft/s limit, 12 ft/s reserve (CS/FAR 25.473, 25.723 [3], [4]); spring \(\eta \approx 0.5\), oleo 0.75–0.9; \(\eta_T \approx 0.47\); \(N_g\) 2–3; add 25 mm; \(P \approx 12.4\) MPa.

Stowage and power

Actuators, pumps and cables

\[ A = \frac{F}{p}, \qquad P = p\,Q, \qquad I = \frac{P}{V} \]
  • Folded leg moves the axle inboard by its length. Emergency extension and aural warning (CS/FAR 25.729 [3], [4]). 3000 psi = 20.7 MPa, 5000 psi = 34.5 MPa; 28 V DC, 115 V 400 Hz AC.

The cabin

Pressure, stress and fresh air

\[ \Delta p = p_{\text{cabin}} - p(h), \qquad \sigma_{\text{hoop}} = \frac{\Delta p\,r}{t} \] \[ \dot m = n(0.00416\ \text{kg/s}) \] \[ P = \frac{\dot m c_p T_1}{\eta_c}\left[\left(\frac{p_{\text{cabin}}}{p_1}\right)^{0.286} - 1\right] \]
  • Cabin ≤ 8000 ft (CS/FAR 25.841); 0.55 lb/min per occupant (25.831) [3], [4]. Ram rise \(p_1 = p(1 + 0.2M^2)^{3.5}\).

Common mistakes

  • Which CG. Main load and tip-back at the aft CG; nose load and tip-over at the forward CG.
  • Per leg or per wheel. Divide by the legs, then by the wheels on each.
  • Tire statistics. Load in pounds, size in inches.
  • The braking rating. 1.5 × rated, nose tires only.
  • Hoop stress. Radius, not diameter.
