How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A reads early aircraft through their numbers, Part B covers streamlining and speed, and Part C covers range and sizing. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units for answers unless a problem asks otherwise, \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(\rho = 1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3\) at sea level, answers to 4 significant figures. Historical data are quoted in Loftin's US units: convert with \(1\ \text{lb} = 0.4536\ \text{kg}\), \(1\ \text{ft}^2 = 0.0929\ \text{m}^2\), \(1\ \text{hp} = 0.7457\ \text{kW}\), \(1\ \text{mph} = 0.44704\ \text{m/s}\). The Formula Sheet is allowed.
