How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers the static margin, the pitching-moment contributions and the neutral point, Part B trim, control and the scissor plot, and Part C directional and lateral stability and engine failure. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units, answers to 4 significant figures: lengths in m, speeds in m/s, pressures in Pa, and \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(\rho_0 = 1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3\). Derivatives are per radian, and positions along the mean aerodynamic chord are fractions of it from its leading edge (or percentages where a problem says so); convert angles in degrees to radians before using them in a derivative. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
