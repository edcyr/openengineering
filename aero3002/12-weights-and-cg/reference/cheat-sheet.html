Weights

The weight build-up

\[ \Wo = \We + W_{\text{crew}} + W_{\text{payload}} + W_f \]
  • Operating weight: \(\We\) + crew. Zero-fuel: + payload (its maximum limits wing bending). Landing: limits gear and structure.
  • Approximate, transports: wing 48.8 kg/m² exposed, tails 26.9, fuselage 24.4 per m² wetted; gear \(0.043\Wo\); engines \(1.3\times\) dry; rest \(0.17\Wo\) ([1], Table 15.2).

Load factors

Flight and landing (from Module 11)

\[ n_{\text{limit}} = 2.1 + \frac{24\,000}{W + 10\,000}, \quad 2.5 \le n \le 3.8 \] \[ N_z = 1.5\max(n_{\text{limit}},\ n_{\text{gust}}), \qquad N_l = 1.5\,N_{\text{gear}} \]
  • \(W\) in pounds (14 CFR 25.337 [3]); factor of safety 1.5 (25.303 [3]). Business jet: 3.048 (4.573), gust 3.255 (4.882): its weights use 4.882; the illustrative aircraft keep the maneuvering factor.
  • +10% \(N_z\): wing +5.5%, fuselage +4.9%, fin +5.2%, nacelles +1.1%, tailplane +1.0%; +10% \(N_l\): gear +2.4%, +1.9%.

Wing and tails (lb, ft)

Wing, horizontal and vertical tails [1]

\[ \begin{aligned} W_{\text{wing}} = 0.0051(W_{dg}N_z)^{0.557}S_w^{0.649}A^{0.5} \\ \times(t/c)^{-0.4}(1+\lambda)^{0.1}(\cos\Lambda)^{-1}S_{csw}^{0.1} \end{aligned} \] \[ \begin{aligned} W_{\text{ht}} = 0.0379K_{uht}(1 + F_w/B_h)^{-0.25}W_{dg}^{0.639}N_z^{0.10}S_{ht}^{0.75} \\ \times L_t^{-1}K_y^{0.704}(\cos\Lambda_{ht})^{-1}A_h^{0.166}(1 + S_e/S_{ht})^{0.1} \end{aligned} \] \[ \begin{aligned} W_{\text{vt}} = 0.0026(1 + H_t/H_v)^{0.225}W_{dg}^{0.556}N_z^{0.536}L_t^{-0.5}S_{vt}^{0.5} \\ \times K_z^{0.875}(\cos\Lambda_{vt})^{-1}A_v^{0.35}(t/c)^{-0.5} \end{aligned} \]
  • \(K_y \approx 0.3L_t\), \(K_z \approx L_t\); \(K_{uht} = 1.143\) all-moving; \(H_t/H_v = 1\) for a T-tail (fin \(\times 1.169\)).
  • Each exponent is the % change in weight per % change in its variable.

Fuselage and gear (lb, ft, in, kt)

Fuselage, main and nose gear [1]

\[ \begin{aligned} W_{\text{fus}} = 0.3280K_{\text{door}}K_{lg}(W_{dg}N_z)^{0.5} \\ \times L^{0.25}S_f^{0.302}(1+K_{ws})^{0.04}(L/D)^{0.10} \end{aligned} \] \[ K_{ws} = 0.75\,\frac{1+2\lambda}{1+\lambda}\,\frac{B_w\tan\Lambda}{L} \] \[ W_{\text{main}} = 0.0106K_{mp}W_l^{0.888}N_l^{0.25}L_m^{0.4}N_{mw}^{0.321}N_{mss}^{-0.5}V_s^{0.1} \] \[ W_{\text{nose}} = 0.032K_{np}W_l^{0.646}N_l^{0.2}L_n^{0.5}N_{nw}^{0.45} \]
  • \(K_{\text{door}}\): 1.0, 1.06 one side door, 1.12 two or clamshell, 1.25 both; \(K_{lg} = 1.12\) gear on fuselage; kneeling 1.126, 1.15.

Propulsion and equipment (lb)

Selected groups [1]

\[ W_{ec} = 2.331W_{\text{engine}}^{0.901}K_pK_{tr} \] \[ \begin{aligned} W_{\text{nac}} = 0.6724K_{ng}N_{Lt}^{0.10}N_w^{0.294}N_z^{0.119} \\ \times W_{ec}^{0.611}N_{en}^{0.984}S_n^{0.224} \end{aligned} \] \[ W_{\text{fuel sys}} = 2.405V_t^{0.606}\left(1+\tfrac{V_i}{V_t}\right)^{-1}\left(1+\tfrac{V_p}{V_t}\right)N_t^{0.5} \] \[ W_{\text{avionics}} = 1.73W_{uav}^{0.983} \]

Calibration and sizing

Calibration factor and the loop

\[ \begin{gathered} k = \frac{W_{e,\text{target}} - W_{\text{catalog}}}{W_{\text{statistical}}} \\[6pt] \Wo = W_{\text{crew}} + W_{\text{payload}} + \frac{W_f}{\Wo}\Wo + \We(\Wo) \end{gathered} \]
  • Composites \(\times\): wing 0.85–0.90, tails 0.83–0.88, fuselage and nacelles 0.90–0.95, gear 0.95–1.0 ([1], Table 15.4).

Center of gravity and the wing

CG, % MAC and the wing shift

\[ \begin{gathered} x_{cg} = \frac{\sum W_ix_i}{\sum W_i}, \qquad \%\,\text{MAC} = 100\,\frac{x_{cg} - x_{\text{LE,MAC}}}{\cbar} \\[6pt] \Delta x = \frac{x_{cg} - x_{\text{LE,MAC}} - p\,\cbar}{1 - W_{wg}/W} \end{gathered} \]
  • Wing CG 30% MAC; tails 30% chord at 35% span; fuselage 45% of \(\lF\) [6]. Load crew, rows front and back first, baggage, fuel.

Common mistakes

  • Units. The equations take lb, ft, ft², in (gear), kt, gal; convert back to kg.
  • Ultimate, not limit. \(N_z = 1.5\,n_{\text{limit}}\).
  • Gear weight. The landing weight \(W_l\), not \(\Wo\).
  • Calibration. Catalog engines are not scaled by \(k\).
  • Moving the wing. The MAC moves by all of \(\Delta x\).
