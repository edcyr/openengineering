How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on five Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers orbital energy, orbits and maneuvers, Part B the ascent and staging, and Part C engines, tanks and closing the design. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units, answers to 4 significant figures: radii in m from Earth's center, speeds in m/s, pressures in Pa inside a formula, with \(\mu = 3.986\times10^{14}\ \text{m}^3/\text{s}^2\), \(R_E = 6378\ \text{km}\) and \(g_0 = 9.80665\ \text{m/s}^2\). Leave out Earth's rotation unless a problem includes it. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
