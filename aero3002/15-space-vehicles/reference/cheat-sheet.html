Orbits

Circular and elliptical orbits

\[ v_c = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r}}, \qquad T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}, \qquad v = \sqrt{\mu\left(\frac{2}{r} - \frac{1}{a}\right)} \] \[ a = \frac{r_p + r_a}{2}, \quad e = \frac{r_a - r_p}{r_a + r_p}, \quad \varepsilon = -\frac{\mu}{2a}, \quad r_pv_p = r_av_a \] \[ \dot\Omega = -\tfrac32 nJ_2\left(\frac{R_E}{a(1 - e^2)}\right)^2\cos i, \qquad n = \sqrt{\mu/a^3} \]
  • \(\mu = 3.986\times10^{14}\ \text{m}^3/\text{s}^2\), \(R_E = 6378\) km, \(J_2 = 1.08263\times10^{-3}\), \(\omega_E = 7.2921\times10^{-5}\) rad/s, \(g_0 = 9.80665\ \text{m/s}^2\). \(r = R_E + h\), in meters.
  • 500 km: 7613 m/s, 94.61 min, 33.52 MJ/kg from the surface. GEO: 35 786 km, 3075 m/s. Sun-synchronous: node 0.9856° east a day, 97.40° at 500 km.

Maneuvers

Hohmann transfer, plane changes, phasing

\[ \Delta v_1 = v_p - v_{c1}, \quad \Delta v_2 = v_{c2} - v_a, \quad t = \pi\sqrt{a_t^3/\mu} \] \[ \Delta v = 2v\sin\frac{\Delta i}{2}, \qquad \Delta v = \sqrt{v_1^2 + v_2^2 - 2v_1v_2\cos\Delta i} \] \[ T_{ph} = T_0\left(1 - \frac{\Delta\theta}{360^\circ}\right), \qquad \Delta v = 2(v_c - v_a) \]
  • 500 to 700 km: 108.3 m/s. 300 km to GEO: 3893 m/s, 5.28 h. 1° at 500 km: 132.9 m/s. Change planes at apogee, combined with a burn.

Getting to orbit

Ascent budget and Earth's rotation

\[ \Delta v = v_{\text{rel}} + \Delta v_g + \Delta v_D + \Delta v_s, \qquad \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{T - D}{m} - g\sin\gamma \] \[ v_E = \omega_ER_E\cos\phi, \quad \sin A_z = \frac{\cos i}{\cos\phi} \] \[ v_{\text{rel}} = \sqrt{v^2 - 2vv_E\sin A_z + v_E^2} \]
  • Typical losses: gravity 1.0–1.5, drag 0.05–0.2, steering 0.05–0.2 km/s. Direct launch needs \(i \ge \phi\).
  • Small launcher, SSO from 45°: \(A_z = 190.5^\circ\), +66.7 m/s; \(9199 = 7679 + 1300 + 120 + 100\) m/s.

The rocket equation and staging

Mass ratio, structural coefficient, optimal split

\[ \Delta v = c\ln\frac{m_0}{m_f}, \quad c = I_{sp}g_0, \quad \varepsilon = \frac{m_s}{m_s + m_p}, \quad \Delta v_{\max} = c\ln\frac{1}{\varepsilon} \] \[ \frac{m_{pl}}{m_0} = \left(\frac{1 - n\varepsilon}{n(1 - \varepsilon)}\right)^N, \qquad n_i = \frac{c_i\eta - 1}{c_i\varepsilon_i\eta}, \quad \sum c_i\ln n_i = \Delta v \] \[ m_p = m\frac{(n - 1)(1 - \varepsilon)}{1 - n\varepsilon}, \qquad m_s = \frac{\varepsilon\,m_p}{1 - \varepsilon} \quad \text{(top down)} \]
  • Small launcher (300 s/0.08, 340 s/0.10): \(\Delta v_1 = 4354\), \(\Delta v_2 = 4846\) m/s; 9710 + 844 and 1546 + 172 kg; 12 572 kg, 41.9 kg per kg of payload. Closed loop: 12 087 kg (\(\varepsilon\) 0.0824, 0.0920).

Engines and propellants

Thrust, specific impulse, nozzle

\[ T = \dot mv_e + (p_e - p_a)A_e = \dot mc = C_Fp_cA_t, \qquad I_{sp} = \frac{c^*C_F}{g_0} \] \[ C_F = C_{F,\text{vac}} - \frac{p_a}{p_c}\frac{A_e}{A_t}, \qquad \rho_{\text{bulk}} = \frac{1 + O/F}{(O/F)/\rho_O + 1/\rho_F} \]
  • Typical vacuum/sea-level \(I_{sp}\) and bulk density: LOX/RP-1 340/300 s, 1025; LOX/CH4 360/320, 829; LOX/LH2 450/390, 362; NTO/MMH 320/280, 1158; solid 285/260, 1800 kg/m³.
  • First-stage engine (\(c^* = 1760\), \(\gamma = 1.24\), 8 MPa, \(\varepsilon_e = 14\)): 283/315 s; upper stage \(\varepsilon_e = 85\): 340 s. Flow separates below about \(p_e = 0.4\,p_a\).

Structures (NASA-STD-5001B)

Factors, margins, tanks, buckling

\[ MS = \frac{\text{allowable}}{\text{limit} \times FS} - 1, \qquad \sigma_{\text{hoop}} = \frac{pR}{t} = 2\sigma_{\text{axial}} \] \[ \sigma_{cr} = \frac{\gamma Et}{R\sqrt{3(1 - \nu^2)}}, \qquad \gamma = 1 - 0.901(1 - e^{-\phi}) \] \[ \phi = \frac{\sqrt{R/t}}{16} \]
  • Metallic: ultimate 1.4; yield 1.0 (prototype) or 1.25 (protoflight); tests 1.4 or 1.2; proof 1.05 (tanks). Composite 1.4 or 1.5; 2.0 at discontinuities. Aircraft: 1.5. Buckling at ultimate load, with a knockdown from SP-8007 [9]. Source: [6].
  • Stage-1 LOX tank: 385.5 kPa, needs 0.82 mm, minimum gauge 1.5 mm; buckling at burnout \(MS = 0.30\).

Sizing loop and license (14 CFR 450)

  • Loop: \(\Delta v\) → staging → engines (\(m = T_{\text{vac}}/(g_0\,T/W_{\text{eng}})\)) → tanks (×1.6) → residuals 1% + allowances → new \(\varepsilon\).
  • 450.101 [7]: \(E_C \le 10^{-4}\) (public), \(2\times10^{-4}\) (neighboring operations); \(P_C \le 10^{-6}\) per launch (\(10^{-5}\)); aircraft \(10^{-6}\). Conditional \(E_C \gt 10^{-3}\) in a phase: flight abort (450.108) or demonstrated reliability. Debris notice: 97% containment.
  • 450.169: inhabitable objects \(10^{-6}\) or 200 km; others \(10^{-5}\) or 25 (2.5 for debris) km. 450.171: passivate stages left in orbit. Deorbit 500 km → 50 km perigee: 129.8 m/s.

Common mistakes

  • Altitude for radius. Orbits use \(r = R_E + h\) from Earth's center, in meters.
  • Seconds for velocity. \(c = I_{sp}\,g_0\): never put seconds into the exponent of the rocket equation.
  • Structural coefficient. \(\varepsilon\) excludes the payload and the stages above; \(m_0/m_f \ne 1/\varepsilon\) once there is a payload.
  • Vacuum or sea level. \(T = T_{\text{vac}} - p_aA_e\); an upper-stage nozzle cannot fire at sea level.
  • Average risk. \(E_C\) below \(10^{-4}\) does not excuse a phase with conditional casualties above \(10^{-3}\).
