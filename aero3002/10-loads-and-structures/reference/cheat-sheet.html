Structural layout

The wing box and a pressure bulkhead

\[ w = (x_{rs} - x_{fs})\,c, \qquad h \approx 0.9\,(t/c)\,c, \qquad A_{\text{box}} = wh \] \[ F_{\text{bulkhead}} = \Delta p\,\frac{\pi d^2}{4}, \qquad c(y) = c_r\left[1 - (1 - \lambda)\frac{2y}{b}\right] \]
  • Semimonocoque: skin (shear, pressure), stringers (bending), frames and ribs (shape, panel length, load introduction). Box: panels and caps carry bending, webs shear, the closed box torsion.
  • Business jet: spars 15% and 60%; ribs 0.6 m, frames 0.5 m (assumed). Box at the side of the fuselage (\(y = 0.80\) m, \(c = 2.579\) m): 1.161 by 0.279 m. Wing tanks 2.05 m³ to 85% of the half span; aft bulkhead 92.1 kN.

Design loads and margins

Load factor, ultimate load, margin of safety

\[ n = \frac{L}{W}, \qquad n_{\text{turn}} = \frac{1}{\cos\phi}, \qquad n_{\text{pull-up}} = 1 + \frac{V^2}{gR} \] \[ \text{ultimate} = 1.5 \times \text{special factor} \times \text{limit} \] \[ MS = \frac{\text{allowable}}{\text{stress} \times \text{factors}} - 1 \ \ge 0 \]
  • Limit: no detrimental permanent deformation; ultimate: no failure for 3 s (25.301 to 25.305). Fitting factor 1.15 (25.625); castings 1.25 to 2.0 (25.621).
  • Allowable at ultimate: \(\min(\sigma_u, 1.5\,\sigma_y)\); the ultimate check governs when \(\sigma_y/\sigma_u > 2/3\). Allowables: 99% / 95% confidence (single path), 90% / 95% (redundant) (25.613).
  • Pressure: \(1.33\times\) relief-valve setting, a limit load (25.365(d)): \(\times 1.5\) at ultimate. Hoop stress \(\Delta p\,r/t\).

The V-n diagram

Limit load factors and design speeds (equivalent airspeed)

\[ n_{\text{limit}} = 2.1 + \frac{24\,000}{W_{\text{lb}} + 10\,000}, \qquad 2.5 \le n \le 3.8 \] \[ V_{S1} = \sqrt{\frac{2(W/S)}{\rho_0\,C_{L\max}}}, \qquad V_A = V_{S1}\sqrt{n_{\text{limit}}} \] \[ V_D \ge \frac{V_C}{0.8}, \qquad M_D \ge M_C + 0.07 \] \[ V_E = V\sqrt{\sigma} = Ma\sqrt{\sigma} \]
  • Negative: \(-1\) to \(V_C\), 0 at \(V_D\); \(V_G\) where the negative stall reaches \(-1\). Corners A, D, E, F, G. Flaps: \(n = 2.0\) to \(V_F \ge 1.8\,V_{S0}\) (25.345).
  • Business jet: \(n = 3.048\); \(V_{S1}\) 55.16, \(V_A\) 96.3, \(V_G\) 70.45, \(V_C\) 138.9 (270 kt), \(V_D\) 173.6 m/s (337.5 kt); \(M_C\) 0.80 above 10.0 km, \(M_D\) 0.87 above 8.1 km.

Gust loads

Pratt's formula

\[ n = 1 \pm \frac{K_g\,\rho_0\,U_{de}\,V_E\,a}{2(W/S)}, \qquad K_g = \frac{0.88\,\mu}{5.3 + \mu} \] \[ \mu = \frac{2(W/S)}{\rho\,\bar c\,a\,g}, \qquad \bar c = \frac{S}{b} \] \[ V_B \ge V_{S1}\sqrt{1 + \Delta n_{\text{ref}}(V_C)}, \qquad V_C \ge V_B + 1.32\,U_{\text{ref}} \]
  • \(W/S\) in N/m²; \(\rho\) at altitude; \(a\) at the flight Mach. US form: \(\Delta n = K_g U_{de} V_E a/(498\,W/S)\), knots, ft/s, lb/ft². \(V_C\) rule in knots with \(U_{\text{ref}}\) in ft/s.
  • \(U_{de}\) to 20 000 ft: 66 (\(V_B\)), 50 (\(V_C\)), 25 ft/s (\(V_D\)), falling linearly to 38, 25, 12.5 at 50 000 ft (Part 23 to 2017). \(U_{\text{ref}}\) (25.341): 56 ft/s at sea level, 44 at 15 000, 20.86 at 60 000 ft; today's transport rule is a dynamic tuned-gust analysis.
  • Business jet, takeoff weight, \(V_C\): sea level \(n = 2.985\); 20 000 ft \(\mu = 79.2\), \(K_g = 0.825\), \(a = 5.127\), \(n = 3.255\): \(N_z = 4.882\). Zero fuel: 4.083. \(V_B\) 99.0 m/s (192.5 kt); least \(V_C\) about 265 kt.

Balanced maneuvers

Wing and tail loads (25.331(b))

\[ L_t = \frac{nW\,(x_{cg} - x_{ac}) + M_{ac}}{l_t}, \qquad L_w = nW - L_t \] \[ M_{ac} = qS\bar c\,C_{M,ac}, \qquad C_{M,ac} = c_{m,0}\,\frac{A\cos^2\Lambda}{A + 2\cos\Lambda} \]
  • Up and nose-up positive; \(x_{cg} - x_{ac} > 0\) for an aft CG. Download largest at high speed with the CG forward; upload at high \(n\), low speed, CG aft.
  • Business jet: \(C_{M,ac} = -0.067\) (\(c_{m,0} = -0.10\), assumed), \(l_t = 7.285\) m, CG 15 to 35% MAC (Module 8). A, CG fwd: \(L_t = -8.69\), \(L_w = 216.2\) kN; D: \(-15.32\) and 222.9 kN; gust up: \(L_w = 233.9\) kN.

Wing shear and bending

Schrenk; shear and bending from the tip

\[ \ell(y) = \frac{L_w}{b}\,\frac{c + c_e}{2\bar c}, \qquad c_e = \frac{4S}{\pi b}\sqrt{1 - \eta^2} \] \[ V(y) = \int_y^{b/2} \left(\ell - n g\,m'\right) d\eta, \qquad M(y) = \int_y^{b/2} V\,d\eta \]
  • By hand: each load outboard of the station times its arm. Elliptic lift per half acts at \(4(b/2)/3\pi\). Fuel in the wing relieves bending, payload does not: the maximum zero-fuel weight.
  • Business jet (wing 555 kg, assumed; wing tanks 1591 kg): lift 85.6% outboard of the side of the fuselage at 2.824 m; structure 84.7% at 2.837 m; fuel at 2.157 m. At the side of the fuselage, limit: A at takeoff 190.2 kN·m; gust at takeoff 206.6; gust at zero fuel 226.9 kN·m and 80.4 kN (the design case).

Sizing the wing box

Panels and webs at ultimate load

\[ N = \frac{M_u}{hw}, \qquad t_{\text{lower}} = \frac{N}{\sigma_{\text{allow}}}, \qquad \sigma_{\text{upper}} = F\sqrt{\frac{NE}{L}} \] \[ t_{\text{upper}} = \frac{N}{\sigma_{\text{upper}}}, \qquad t_{\text{webs}} = \frac{V_u}{\tau_{\text{allow}}\,h} \] \[ m = 2\int \rho\,w\,(t_u + t_l)\,dy \]
  • \(F \approx 0.81\), Z-stringer panels (Farrar; recalled); \(L\) = rib spacing; \(\sigma_{\text{upper}}\) no more than the compressive allowable. \(\tau_{\text{allow}} \approx 0.6\,\sigma_u\) for aluminium.
  • Business jet, 7075-T6, ribs 0.6 m: \(M_u = 340.4\) kN·m, \(N = 1053\) N/mm; lower 1.84 mm, upper 3.66 mm at 287 MPa; webs 1.26 mm in all (minimum gauge). Bending material 130.7 kg (lower 38.1, upper 92.6), about a fifth of Module 11's 644 kg wing. 7075/2024 137.2; Al-Li 121.8; CFRP 77.3; Ti 154.0; steel 185.0 kg.

Common mistakes

  • Newtons per square meter. \(W/S\) in the stall-speed and gust formulas is a pressure: kg/m² times \(g\).
  • Pounds in 25.337. \(W\) in the limit load factor is in pounds; then check the bounds 2.5 and 3.8.
  • Equivalent airspeed. The envelope is in \(V_E\); \(\mu\) uses the density at altitude and \(a\) the true Mach number.
  • Limit or ultimate. Size at ultimate: 1.5 times limit, times any fitting factor. The 1.33 pressure factor is a limit load.
  • The critical weight. The gust load factor is largest at the lightest weight, but \(nW\) at the heaviest: \(N_z\) is taken at the takeoff weight. The wing's bending is largest at zero fuel, because its fuel relief is gone.
