How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on four Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A covers materials, the wing box, margins of safety and load factors, Part B the V-n diagram, gusts and balanced tail loads, and Part C wing bending and the wing box's panels. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units, answers to 4 significant figures: lengths in m, speeds in m/s (equivalent airspeeds, with 1 knot = 0.5144 m/s), forces in N, stresses in MPa, and \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), \(\rho_0 = 1.225\ \text{kg/m}^3\).
