How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space, and Problem 7 gets blank axes for a sketch. It fits on about five Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A is rectangular components and projectiles, Part B is path coordinates and curvature, and Part C connects the two and applies them. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

Unless a problem says otherwise: \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), no air resistance, \(x\) horizontal and \(y\) vertically up, answers to 4 significant figures with units. Give \(a_t\) a sign: positive when the particle speeds up, negative when it slows down. \(a_n = v^2/\rho\) is never negative; say which way it points. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
