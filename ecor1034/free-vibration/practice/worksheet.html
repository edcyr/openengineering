How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or five A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A is harmonic motion, Part B spring–mass systems, and Part C pendulums and energy. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

Unless a problem says otherwise: \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\); bodies are treated as particles, springs are light and linear, and there is no friction or damping; angles inside sines and cosines are in radians. Give answers to 4 significant figures with units. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
