Typing answers

Answer boxes understand arithmetic, so you can type the expression you wrote on paper instead of working it out on a calculator first. Each box shows how it read your input (for example = 23.16), so you can catch a typo before you check.

Give each answer in the unit shown next to its box: meters, seconds, hertz, radians per second, newtons per meter, newtons, joules or degrees. Angles inside sin and cos are in radians; phase angles and pendulum angles are asked for in degrees. Signs matter where the problem names a positive direction.

Tip

Typing the whole expression is faster and safer than rounding at each step: 2*pi*sqrt(2/800) is exact, while a rounded square root carried into later steps can drift past the 1% tolerance.
