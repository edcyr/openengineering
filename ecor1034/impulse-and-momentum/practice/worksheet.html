How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A is momentum, impulse and the principle of impulse and momentum, Part B is forces that vary with time and conservation of momentum, and Part C is impact and problems in stages. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

Unless a problem says otherwise: \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\); bodies are treated as particles; choose a positive direction for each axis and keep the sign of every velocity and impulse, so a velocity in the other direction is negative; \(e\) is the coefficient of restitution. Give answers to 4 significant figures with units. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
