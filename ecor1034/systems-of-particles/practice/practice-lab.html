Typing answers

Answer boxes understand arithmetic, so you can type the expression you wrote on paper instead of working it out on a calculator first. Each box shows how it read your input (for example = 333.8), so you can catch a typo before you check.

What you can type
You typeIt reads
4.02*sqrt(2*9.81*0.1405)/0.02\(\frac{4.02\sqrt{2(9.81)(0.1405)}}{0.02} = 333.8\)
1.5*(1-cos(25°))\(1.5(1 - \cos 25^\circ) = 0.1405\)
sqrt(20^2+15^2) or √(20^2+15^2)\(\sqrt{625} = 25\)
(2*6+3*(-2))/5\((12 - 6)/5 = 1.2\)
-1.68\(-1.68\): a velocity to the left, when right is positive
atan(15/8)\(\tan^{-1}(1.875)\), in radians: for an angle answer, type it in degrees, e.g. 61.93

Give each answer in the unit shown next to its box: meters per second, meters per second squared, newtons, newton-seconds, joules, meters or degrees; a coefficient of restitution has no unit. Signs matter where the problem names a positive direction: type the minus sign for a velocity or an acceleration the other way. Inside cos, sin and tan, mark degrees with °; a bare number there is read as radians.

Tip

Typing the whole expression is faster and safer than rounding at each step: (2*6+3*(-2))/5 is exact, while rounding a common velocity first and then squaring it for the energy can drift past the 1% tolerance.
