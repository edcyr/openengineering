Self-Check Quiz

Fifteen questions on the core skills of Lessons 1–9: rectangular components, projectiles, path coordinates, radius of curvature and converting between systems. Submit to see your score and a worked solution for every question.

How it works

  • Work on your own with a calculator and scrap paper. Try it without notes first: that shows you what you really know.
  • Nothing is marked until you press Submit quiz. Until then you can move between questions and change any answer.
  • Each question is either right or wrong. When a question has several boxes, every box must be right.
  • After you submit, you see your score, a review of every question with a worked solution, and a results sheet you can print or save as a PDF.
  • Use \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\) and ignore air resistance unless a question says otherwise.

Each box shows how it read your entry (for example = 7.81), so you can check it before you submit. Type only the number; the unit is shown next to the box.

How close is close enough?

Answers are accepted within \(1\%\) of the exact value, so three or four significant figures are plenty. Typing the calculation itself, such as sqrt(2^2+2.5^2), avoids rounding errors. Watch the sign of \(a_t\): it is negative when the particle slows down.
