How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A is positions, unit vectors and velocity, Part B is acceleration and guided paths, and Part C is kinetics: equations of motion, guides and central forces. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

Unless a problem says otherwise: \(\theta\) is measured counter-clockwise from the \(+x\) axis, in radians in every formula (so rates are in rad/s); positions use \(r \ge 0\) and \(0 \le \theta < 2\pi\); \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\); the unit vectors are \(\er\), \(\et\), \(\ez\). Give exact values where they exist, then decimals to 4 significant figures, with units on every answer. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
