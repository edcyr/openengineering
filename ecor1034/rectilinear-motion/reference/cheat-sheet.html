Formula Sheet

The calculus rules and the kinematic relations that every solution in the module is built from. Conventions: Hibbeler notation (position \(s\) from a fixed origin \(O\), velocity \(v\), acceleration \(a\), constant acceleration \(a_c\), relative quantities \(s_{B/A}\)); one positive direction, chosen and kept; angles in radians; SI units with \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\).

Fits on two pages of Letter or A4. For a digital copy, choose “Save as PDF” as the printer.

Derivatives

Derivatives of the basic functions
\(\dfrac{d}{dt}\,t^n = n\,t^{n-1}\)\(\dfrac{d}{dt}\,c = 0\)
\(\dfrac{d}{dt}\sin kt = k\cos kt\)\(\dfrac{d}{dt}\cos kt = -k\sin kt\)
\(\dfrac{d}{dt}\,e^{kt} = k\,e^{kt}\)\(\dfrac{d}{dt}\ln t = \dfrac{1}{t}\)

Product, quotient and chain rules

\[ (fg)' = f\,'g + fg' \qquad \left(\frac{f}{g}\right)' = \frac{f\,'g - fg'}{g^2} \]\[ \frac{d}{dt}f\big(u(t)\big) = \frac{df}{du}\frac{du}{dt} \]

The derivative is the slope of the tangent and the instantaneous rate of change. A peak or a valley has a zero derivative.

Integrals

Integrals of the basic functions
\(\int t^n\,dt = \dfrac{t^{n+1}}{n + 1} + C\) (\(n \ne -1\))\(\int \dfrac{dt}{t} = \ln\lvert t\rvert + C\)
\(\int \sin kt\,dt = -\dfrac{\cos kt}{k} + C\)\(\int \cos kt\,dt = \dfrac{\sin kt}{k} + C\)
\(\int e^{kt}\,dt = \dfrac{e^{kt}}{k} + C\)\(\int \dfrac{dt}{a + bt} = \dfrac{\ln\lvert a + bt\rvert}{b} + C\)

With limits

\[ \int_a^b f\,dt = F(b) - F(a) \]

From a known start

\[ s = s_0 + \int_0^t v\,dt \]

\(C\) is fixed by an initial condition. A definite integral is a signed area: below the axis counts negative. Separate the variables: \(dv/dt = g(v) \Rightarrow \int dv/g(v) = t\).

Rectilinear kinematics

For every rectilinear motion

\[ \colVel{v} = \frac{ds}{dt} \qquad \colAcc{a} = \frac{dv}{dt} = \frac{d^2s}{dt^2} \qquad a\,ds = v\,dv \]
  • Displacement \(\Delta s = s_2 - s_1\) (signed); distance traveled \(s_T\) adds every leg as positive: find where \(v = 0\) first.
  • \(v_{\text{avg}} = \Delta s/\Delta t\); average speed \(= s_T/\Delta t\).
  • Speeding up: \(v\) and \(a\) have the same sign; slowing down: opposite signs.

Constant acceleration

Only when \(a = a_c\) is constant

\[ v = v_0 + a_c t \qquad s = s_0 + v_0 t + \tfrac12 a_c t^2 \]\[ v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a_c(s - s_0) \]

Each leaves out one quantity (\(s\), \(v\) or \(t\)). Free fall, up positive: \(a_c = -g = -9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), also at the top. Split a motion into stages of constant \(a\); the end of one is the start of the next.

Variable acceleration

Which relation to integrate
GivenIntegrate
\(a = f(t)\)\(\int_{v_0}^{v} dv = \int_0^t f\,dt\), then \(\int_{s_0}^{s} ds = \int_0^t v\,dt\)
\(a = f(v)\)\(\int_{v_0}^{v} \dfrac{dv}{f(v)} = t\) or \(\int_{v_0}^{v} \dfrac{v\,dv}{f(v)} = s - s_0\)
\(a = f(s)\)\(\int_{v_0}^{v} v\,dv = \int_{s_0}^{s} f\,ds\)

Drag: terminal velocity where \(a = 0\). Spring \(a = -\omega^2 s\): \(v^2 = v_0^2 - \omega^2(s^2 - s_0^2)\).

Erratic motion: graphs

  • Slopes, down the chain: \(v\) = slope of \(s\)–\(t\); \(a\) = slope of \(v\)–\(t\).
  • Areas, up the chain: \(\Delta s\) = area under \(v\)–\(t\); \(\Delta v\) = area under \(a\)–\(t\).
  • \(a\)–\(s\) graph: \(\tfrac12(v_2^2 - v_1^2)\) = area under it. \(v\)–\(s\) graph: \(a = v\,dv/ds\).
  • Constant \(a\): straight \(v\)–\(t\), parabolic \(s\)–\(t\). Zero \(a\): level \(v\)–\(t\), straight \(s\)–\(t\).

Absolute dependent motion

One cord

\[ \textstyle\sum_i k_i s_i = l_T \;\Rightarrow\; \sum_i k_i v_i = 0 \;\Rightarrow\; \sum_i k_i a_i = 0 \]

Measure each \(s_i\) from a fixed datum along particle \(i\)'s line of motion; \(k_i\) counts the segments that change with \(s_i\). A load on \(n\) parts: \(v_B = -v_A/n\). A slanted segment: \(l = \sqrt{x^2 + h^2}\), differentiated with the chain rule. Each cord gives one equation.

Relative motion

Along one line

\[ s_B = s_A + s_{B/A} \quad\;\; v_B = v_A + v_{B/A} \quad\;\; a_B = a_A + a_{B/A} \]

Same origin and positive direction for both. \(v_{A/B} = -v_{B/A}\). Level or meeting: \(s_{B/A} = 0\). In an elevator: \(a_{\text{object}/E} = -g - a_E\) (up positive).

Common mistakes

  • Constant-acceleration equations when \(a\) depends on \(t\), \(v\) or \(s\).
  • Displacement for distance when the particle turns back.
  • Degrees in derivatives: \((\sin kt)' = k\cos kt\) needs radians.
  • The chain-rule factor forgotten: \((e^{-2t})' = -2e^{-2t}\).
  • The constant of integration dropped, or not fixed by the initial condition.
  • \(a = 0\) at the top of a vertical throw: it is \(-g\).
  • Areas under \(a\)–\(s\) taken as \(\Delta v\): they give \(\tfrac12\Delta(v^2)\).
  • Coordinates from a moving point in dependent motion.

Symbols and units

  • \(s\) position from the fixed origin \(O\) (m)
  • \(\Delta s,\ s_T\) displacement, distance traveled (m)
  • \(v,\ a\) velocity, acceleration (m/s, m/s²)
  • \(a_c\) a constant acceleration (m/s²)
  • \(g\) acceleration due to gravity, \(9.81\) (m/s²)
  • \(l_T\) total length of a cord (m)
  • \(s_{B/A},\ v_{B/A},\ a_{B/A}\) \(B\) relative to \(A\) (m, m/s, m/s²)
  • \(C\) constant of integration
