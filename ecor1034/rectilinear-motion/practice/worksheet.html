How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A is the calculus refresher, Part B the kinematics of one particle, and Part C graphs, dependent motion and relative motion. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

Unless a problem says otherwise: \(g = 9.81\ ext{m/s}^2\); bodies are treated as particles and air resistance is neglected; angles inside sines and cosines are in radians; choose a positive direction and keep to it. Give answers to 4 significant figures with units. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
