Typing answers

Answer boxes understand arithmetic, so you can type the expression you wrote on paper instead of working it out on a calculator first. Each box shows how it read your input (for example = 7.672), so you can catch a typo before you check.

2e3 or 2000\(2000\)
4.09\(4.09\), a decimal: accepted when it is within 1% of the exact value

Give each answer in the unit shown next to its box: joules, newtons, meters, meters per second or kilowatts. Signs matter for work: a force that opposes the motion does negative work, so type the minus sign. Inside cos, sin and tan, mark degrees with °; a bare number there is read as radians.

Tip

Typing the whole expression is faster and safer than rounding at each step: sqrt(2*9.81*3) is exact, while rounding \(v^2\) first and then taking the root can drift past the 1% tolerance.
