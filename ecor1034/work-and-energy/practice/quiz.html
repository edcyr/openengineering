Self-Check Quiz

How it works

  • Work on your own with a calculator and scrap paper. Try it without notes first: that shows you what you really know.
  • Nothing is marked until you press Submit quiz. Until then you can move between questions and change any answer.
  • Each question is either right or wrong. When a question has several boxes, every box must be right.
  • After you submit, you see your score, a review of every question with a worked solution, and a results sheet you can print or save as a PDF.
  • Use \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\). Give each answer in the unit shown next to its box, and keep the sign of every work: a force that opposes the motion does negative work.

Each box shows how it read your entry (for example = 7.672), so you can check it before you submit. Type only the number or expression; the unit is shown next to the box.

How close is close enough?

Numbers are accepted within \(1\%\) of the exact value, so three or four significant figures are plenty. Typing the whole expression, such as sqrt(2*9.81*3), is always safe: nothing is rounded along the way.

A unit slip is the most common way to lose a mark: kilometers per hour must become meters per second, and an answer asked for in kilowatts must not be typed in watts.
