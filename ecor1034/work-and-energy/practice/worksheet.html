How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A is the work of forces, Part B is the principle of work and energy and power, and Part C is potential energy, conservation of energy and curved paths. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

Unless a problem says otherwise: \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\); bodies are treated as particles; \(y\) is measured upward from the datum you choose; spring deformations \(s\) are measured from the unstretched length; every work keeps its sign, so a force that opposes the motion does negative work. Give answers to 4 significant figures with units. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
