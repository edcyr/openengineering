Typing answers

Answer boxes understand arithmetic, so you can type the expression you wrote on paper instead of working it out on a calculator first. Each box shows how it read your input (for example = 1.962), so you can catch a typo before you check.

What you can type
You typeIt reads
(4-0.2*8)*9.81/12\((4 - 0.2 \times 8)(9.81)/12 = 1.962\)
12*9.81*sin(25°)\(12(9.81)\sin 25^\circ = 49.75\)
sqrt(0.6*9.81*80) or √(0.6*9.81*80)\(\sqrt{470.9} = 21.70\)
1200*(9.81-20^2/50)\(1200(9.81 - 400/50) = 2172\)
-2.5\(-2.5\): an acceleration to the left, when right is positive
atan(25^2/(9.81*120))\(\tan^{-1}(0.531)\), in radians: for an angle answer, type it in degrees, e.g. 27.96

Give each answer in the unit shown next to its box: newtons or kilonewtons, meters per second, meters per second squared, kilograms, seconds, meters or degrees; a coefficient of friction has no unit. Signs matter where the problem names a positive direction: type the minus sign for a velocity or an acceleration the other way. Inside cos, sin and tan, mark degrees with °; a bare number there is read as radians.

Tip

Typing the whole expression is faster and safer than rounding at each step: (4-0.2*8)*9.81/12 is exact, while rounding the friction force first can drift past the 1% tolerance.
