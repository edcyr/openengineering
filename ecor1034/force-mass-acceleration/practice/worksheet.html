How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on six Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A is Newton's second law, free-body diagrams and friction, Part B is cords, pulleys and forces that vary, and Part C is curved paths and systems of particles. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

Unless a problem says otherwise: \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\); bodies are treated as particles; cords are light and inextensible and pulleys light and smooth; choose a positive direction for each axis, and find every normal force from its own equation. Give answers to 4 significant figures with units. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
