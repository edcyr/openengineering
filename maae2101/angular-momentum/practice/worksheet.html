How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on about five Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A is particles and systems, Part B is rigid bodies in plane motion, and Part C is three dimensions and gyroscopes. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units, \(g = 9.81\ \text{m/s}^2\), answers to 4 significant figures. In the plane, angular momentum and moments are counterclockwise positive. Slender rods have negligible thickness, and "light" parts have negligible mass. The Formula Sheet is allowed.
