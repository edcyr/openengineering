How to use this worksheet

  1. Print it, or save it as a PDF. Each problem gets a dotted work space. It fits on about five Letter or A4 pages.
  2. Work on paper. Part A is moments of inertia, Part B is products of inertia and the inertia tensor, and Part C is any axis and principal axes. Each problem names the lesson to review if you get stuck.
  3. Check your final answers with Show answer under each problem, or show them all at once. Only final answers are given here.

Conventions

SI units, answers to 4 significant figures. Products of inertia are defined as \(I_{xy} = \int xy\,dm\), so they can be negative, and they enter the inertia tensor \(\Imat\) with a minus sign. Slender rods and thin plates have negligible thickness. The Formula Sheet, with its table of standard bodies, is allowed.
